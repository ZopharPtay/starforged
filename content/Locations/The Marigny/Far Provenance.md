# Far Provenance

**Type:** Shop (spiritual antiquarian)
**Location:** [[Paxton Orbital Station]]
**Proprietor:** [[Ike Sutton]]

## Summary

A cluttered curio gallery specializing in religious artifacts, devotional objects, and spiritual relics from across the Forge's diverse faiths.  Black iron totems, Primordial devotional carvings, meditation bells from healer-order monasteries, funerary masks from the Founder Clans.  The kind of shop where everything has a story and the owner can tell it.

Not truly a business in the conventional sense.  The shop functions as a front and funding mechanism for Ike's real pursuit: acquiring specific artifacts tied to a topic of religious significance.  Everything else is window dressing that accumulates like driftwood.

## Appearance

Ill-equipped by choice, not poverty.  No proper display cases, no labels, no inventory system.  Shelves are cluttered and overflowing.  Items are grouped by vibes rather than taxonomy.  At a glance it reads as "disorganized but impressive."  The sheer volume suggests a knowledgeable collector.  The truth is simpler: nothing leaves unless someone specifically asks for it.

## Notable Features

- Stock is genuine and often valuable, but completely untracked.  No master inventory or price list exists.
- Ike can appraise anything on the spot (historian's expertise), creating the impression of competence.
- A locked case in the back holds items of more dubious legality: precursor glyphs, relics with forged or impossible provenance.
- The shop's niche market means new faces are rare and notable.

## Vulnerability (hidden)

If robbed selectively, Ike would have difficulty identifying what was taken.  Unless the thieves cleaned him out entirely or took something from his personal interest, he might not notice items missing for days or weeks.  This is not apparent to outsiders; his on-demand expertise masks the gap.
