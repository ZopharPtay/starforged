# Original Sin

**Type:** Night Club
**Location:** [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Paxton Orbital Station|Paxton Orbital Station]]
**Proprietor:** TBD

## Summary

Original Sin occupies a double-height pod on one of the upper rings, gutted and rebuilt as a hedonistic EDM venue.  Bass-heavy electronic music pulses through the floor plates hard enough to feel in your teeth.  Holographic projections wash the walls in cycling neon; the dance floor is packed shoulder-to-shoulder most nights.  Cheap synth-liquor flows fast and nobody cards.

For the station's working poor, it's the release valve.  Spend a shift's wages, lose yourself in the noise and the crowd, stumble home deaf and empty.  The Cartel likes it that way.  Docile populations need an outlet.

## Appearance

Strobing ultraviolet and magenta.  Fog machines running constantly, turning the interior into a humid haze that catches the light.  The ceiling is high enough for catwalks and suspended DJ rigs.  The bar is a long slab of backlit polymer, glowing from within.  VIP booths on the upper level overlook the floor through tinted panels.

## Notable Features

- A back room on the upper level, accessible by invitation only.  Regulars know it's a hangout for a hacker collective called the [[Netrunners]].
- VIP section requires spending credits or knowing someone.  Minor Cartel figures hold permanent tables.
- The sound system is military surplus; repurposed comms array speakers that can push volume well past comfort.
