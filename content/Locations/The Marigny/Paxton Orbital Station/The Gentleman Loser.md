# The Gentleman Loser

**Type:** Dive Bar
**Location:** [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Paxton Orbital Station|Paxton Orbital Station]]
**Proprietor:** TBD

## Summary

Tucked into Ring Five, The Gentleman Loser is a claustrophobic sanctuary for couriers, fixers, and people who want to drink without being noticed.  Cheap synthetic tobacco haze hangs thick in the air, lit by the jitter of obsolete display panels.  Exposed fiber-optic bundles dangle from the ceiling.  The bar top is polished scrap; defunct mainframe chassis bolted together and sealed with resin.  The stools are repurposed hydraulic pistons that hiss faintly when weight settles on them.

Despite the grime, there's a low-frequency energy to the place.  Retro-industrial synth-wave plays from blown speakers, the bass heavy enough to cover conversation at the next table.  Deals happen over cracked holopads in the shadowed booths along the back wall.  Encrypted cred-sticks change hands below table level.  Nobody looks up when the door opens.

## Appearance

The lighting is deliberately bad.  Warm amber from the fiber-optic tangles overhead, cold blue from the display panels behind the bar, and nothing else.  Corners stay dark by design.  The ceiling is low enough to feel pressing.  Exposed conduit and cable runs give the impression the station forgot this pod existed and the bar grew into the gap like fungus.

## Notable Features

- The bartender has a visible cybernetic optical implant (vintage, not medical-grade).  Functional or decorative is an open question.
- Booths along the back wall are high-backed and angled to block sightlines and ensure privacy.
- [[Florin Kai & Basil]] hold a permanent booth in the back corner.
