# Paxton Orbital Station

**Type:** Orbital settlement
**Orbits:** Unnamed jungle world
**Region:** Outlands
**Population:** Thousands
**Authority:** Ineffectual (real authority: [[The Cartel]])
**Projects:** Subsistence, Entertainment

## Summary

The sector's main hub.  A sprawling orbital station above a jungle world.  Big enough to have a port, markets, and diversions, but barely feeds itself.  Official authority exists on paper; the Cartel is the actual governing force.  Cheap entertainment keeps the population docile: bars, gambling, fighting pits, vice.

## Station Addressing

Locations use the format **R{ring}-{floor}-{pod}**.  Example: R5-14-23 = Ring 5, Floor 14, Pod 23.

## Dock Infrastructure

Paxton uses a tiered docking system:

**Public pads (outer ring):**  Active traffic berths with airlock access, fuel lines, and launch clearance.  Short-term parking with a ticking clock.  Visitors key in a permit code indicating business purpose; this gives a few hours before fees start accruing.  These are premium real estate and the station keeps them turning over.

**Service cradles (ring exterior/underside):**  When a shop vouches for a visitor, the station tugs the ship from the public pad to a service cradle associated with that shop's lease.  Cradles are external berths along the ring structure.  Ships in cradle cannot independently launch; they must be shunted back to a public pad for departure.  No dock fees apply here because cradle access is included in the shop's lease.  The station benefits because it keeps active pads clear.

**Mobile gantry:**  A shared mobile gangway services multiple cradles along a ring section.  It can be requested and repositioned to connect a cradle to the ring's interior corridor, providing pressurized access to the ship without EVA.  Takes time to reposition (it services many cradles) and nobody has to be blocking it, but access is available by default.  This allows:
- Ship owners to access their vessel and its contents while in cradle
- Mechanics to wheel tool carts and parts aboard easily
- Cargo loading/unloading without suit work

**Lockout escalation:**  A shop owner (or station authority) *could* override gantry access to a specific cradle, effectively locking someone out of their ship without EVA capability.  But this is an active, hostile choice, not the default state.
