# Orris

**Type:** Jungle World
**Region:** Outlands ([[The Marigny]] sector)
**Atmosphere:** Breathable
**Life:** Bountiful (assumed; jungle world default)
**Orbital Settlement:** [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Paxton Orbital Station|Paxton Orbital Station]]

## Summary

The jungle world below Paxton.  Humid, green, alive.  Most of the population lives on Paxton above; the surface supports landing facilities, suppliers, and whoever prefers gravity and open sky to recycled station air.

## Known Locations

- [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Celadon Terminus|Celadon Terminus]]: The primary shuttle landing station for this region.  Public transit hub.
