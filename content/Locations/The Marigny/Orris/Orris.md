# Orris

**Type:** Jungle World
**Region:** Outlands (The Marigny sector)
**Atmosphere:** Breathable
**Life:** Bountiful and hostile
**Orbital Settlement:** [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Paxton Orbital Station|Paxton Orbital Station]]

## Summary

The jungle world below Paxton.  Humid, green, alive, and aggressively reclaiming every meter humans try to hold.  The surface is not colonized; it is *occupied*.  A single maintained foothold exists at [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Celadon Terminus|Celadon Terminus]], defended around the clock against encroaching flora and hostile fauna.

The population of The Marigny lives on Paxton in orbit because living on Orris is a constant, losing battle against the jungle.

## The Jungle

The vegetation is forgespawn-touched or forgespawn-adjacent.  Growth is relentless, especially at night.  During daylight hours the jungle photosynthesizes; at night it *surges*, growing visibly, reclaiming cleared ground in hours rather than days.

Fauna: something lives in the canopy that is smart, mean, and coordinates.  Whether these are technically "forgespawn" or "extremely aggressive native predators corrupted by chaotic energies" is an academic debate nobody on the perimeter cares about.  Corporate reports call them "indigenous fauna."  Veterans have other names.

## Why Humans Bother

A resource is being extracted that justifies the entire operation.  Nature TBD.  Valuable enough that expeditions go out regularly into the deep jungle with heavy equipment and armed escort.  Whatever comes back gets processed or staged at the compound and launched to orbit via mass driver.

The Cartel controls this operation (or takes their cut of it).

## Logistics

- **Up (mass driver):** Electromagnetic launch rail fires unmanned cargo pods to orbit on a schedule.  No fuel cost, just power.  The thump of a launch shakes the compound every few hours.  The rail is significant infrastructure and additional perimeter to defend.
- **Down (kinetic drops):** Bulk supplies come down as guided cargo containers; no engines, just mass, ablative shielding, and a guidance package.  No parachutes.  The impact crater is half the point: each drop is calculated to land on the perimeter edge where growth is currently worst, doubling as an orbital strike to push back the jungle.  The forest fills the crater within days.
- **Personnel shuttle:** The shuttle between Paxton and Celadon is for bodies only, not cargo.  This is why it's a bus; the heavy logistics bypass humans entirely.

## The Compound Origin (not public knowledge)

The initial clearing was created by thermobaric orbital strike.  The detonation did something to the soil: fused it, killed it, made it hostile to root systems.  This dead ground is exactly what makes holding this location possible; the vines encroach from the edges but don't grow up from below.  The compound sits in a shallow bowl of scorched, sterile earth.  Expansion beyond this dead zone has proven impractical; the jungle reclaims living soil too quickly.

## Defense Rhythm

- **Daytime:** Machete crews on the perimeter.  Maintenance cutting.  Mass driver launches.  Expedition prep.
- **Nighttime:** Flamethrower shifts.  The vine surge accelerates after dark.  The perimeter glows with a ring of fire.  Night is never truly dark inside the compound.  You go to sleep surrounded by firelight.
- **Periodic:** Kinetic drops slamming the perimeter where growth is worst.  Calculated, targeted, deliberate.
