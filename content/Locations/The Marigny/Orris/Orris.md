# Orris

**Type:** Jungle World
**Region:** Outlands ([[Locations/The Marigny/The Marigny|The Marigny]] sector)
**Atmosphere:** Breathable / Ideal
**Life:** Bountiful (forgespawn-infested)
**Orbital Settlement:** [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Paxton Orbital Station|Paxton Orbital Station]]

## Summary

From orbit: a tropical paradise.  Lush, green, breathable, beautiful.  All sensors confirm it.

From the ground: a forgespawn-infested jungle concentrated around an active precursor vault extraction operation.  The sole human foothold is [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Celadon Terminus|Celadon Terminus]], maintained at enormous cost.

## The True History (not public knowledge in full)

### The Vault

A precursor vault exists somewhere on or beneath the surface of Orris.  Long ago, Remnants guarded it.  The forgespawn won that war.  The Remnants are dead (their biomechanical remains may be part of what's being scavenged now).  The forgespawn dispersed across the planet afterward; with no Remnant activity to draw them, they became dormant or scattered thinly through the jungle.  For centuries, Orris was a quiet, beautiful, deadly-only-in-the-normal-ways jungle world.

### The Resort Attempt

A Faction (TBD; one of the Five Founder Clans?) purchased orbital and surface rights.  From-orbit surveys showed paradise.  The plan: exclusive luxury retreat.

What got built:
- Celadon Terminus: cruise-terminal welcome center.  Grand Atrium.  Terrazzo plaza.  Guest-arrival architecture.
- The plaza area: thermobaric-cleared and paved as the resort's main square.  (They were paving it anyway.)
- Partial resort structures beyond the plaza (day spa, at minimum).

The early construction phase went fine.  Occasional wildlife encounters; nothing out of the ordinary for a jungle world.

### The Breach

Someone cracked the vault.  Digging foundations, clearing land, drilling; the exact circumstances are unclear.  They found something valuable inside.  Precursor tech, materials, data; nature TBD.

The breach rang a dinner bell.  Forgespawn across the planet, dormant since the Remnants went silent, suddenly sensed Remnant material being disturbed.  Activity concentrated around the compound.  Escalated fast.

### The Pivot

Construction crews started disappearing.  Then security teams.  By the time anyone connected "we opened something" to "the jungle is trying to kill us," the operation was already pivoting.  Whatever they found in the vault was too valuable to walk away from.  Resort → extraction operation.

### The Present Siege

Every time vault material is brought to the surface and processed, it refreshes the scent.  The extraction perpetuates its own siege.  The forgespawn press in because the operation keeps chumming the water.  If the vault were emptied completely and the operation ceased, the forgespawn would eventually disperse again.  But "eventually" might be decades, and nobody's walking away from money in the ground.

## The Jungle

**Flora:** Growth is relentless, especially at night.  Daytime: photosynthesis.  Nighttime: surge growth.  The accelerated vine expansion is likely forgespawn-influenced flora responding to whatever the breached vault is leaking.  Regular jungle was manageable; the forgespawn-touched growth is what makes it a 24/7 war.

**Fauna:** Forgespawn.  Genuine hostiles; invaders who won their ancient war against the Remnants.  They were dispersed and dormant across Orris for centuries after the Remnants went silent.  The vault breach re-concentrated them around the compound.  They are drawn by the stimulus of Remnant material being disturbed; if extraction ceased, they would eventually disperse again over decades.  But they would not disappear.  They won this planet.  They live here now.  Stopping the extraction removes the dinner bell, not the predators.

**Sensory concealment:** The forgespawn on Orris are invisible to sensors.  Not physically invisible; you can see them with your eyes if you're looking at the right spot.  But scanners, motion detectors, thermal imaging; all of it slides past them.  This is why orbital surveys showed paradise.  The forgespawn were there the whole time; the instruments just couldn't register them.  Ground crews initially dismissed encounters as "of course there are predators, look at the place" because nothing showed up on their equipment.  By the time the pattern was obvious, it was too late.

This also means perimeter defense is low-tech by necessity.  Sensors are useless against the primary threat.  You need eyes.  Human eyes.  Which is why the crews rotate and burn out.

**The connection to Leight:** His Shade gift makes *things* unnoticeable.  The forgespawn make *themselves* unnoticeable to sensors.  Same mechanism?  Different expression of the same energy?  [[Characters/Leight/Leight|Leight]] has never had reason to test whether his gift works on sensors specifically (he's always operated around people, not machines).  If it does, that's a significant upgrade he doesn't know about.

## The Cartel's Role

[[The Cartel]] does NOT own Orris.  They are the Teamsters: logistics, transport, labor management, supply lines.  They keep the shuttle running, the workers in line, the operation flowing.  They take a massive cut.  Their power is being indispensable infrastructure, not holding the deed.

## Logistics

- **Up (mass driver):** Electromagnetic launch rail.  Unmanned cargo pods to orbit.  Thumps every few hours.
- **Down (retro-rocket drops):** Supply containers with retro-rockets that fire before landing, incinerating the immediate zone.  Workers rush to unload before regrowth.  Containers get repurposed as infrastructure (housing, storage, perimeter material).  Nothing wasted.
- **Off-target drops:** They happen.  Occasionally retrieved depending on how far out they landed and what was on them; a close miss with critical supplies might warrant a recovery sortie.  But anything deep in the jungle is a write-off.  The deep canopy is littered with lost containers slowly being consumed.  Salvage hook for the brave or desperate.
- **Personnel shuttle:** Paxton ↔ Celadon.  ~1 hour transit, every ~2 hours.  For bodies only.

## Defense Rhythm

- **Daytime:** Machete crews on the perimeter.  Maintenance cutting.  Mass driver launches.  Expedition prep.
- **Nighttime:** Flamethrower shifts.  The vine surge accelerates after dark.  The perimeter glows with a ring of fire.  Night is never truly dark.  You go to sleep smelling smoke.
- **Supply drops:** Targeted where growth is worst.  Retro-burn clears the zone; the clock starts immediately.

## The Abandoned Resort

Just past the current perimeter edge: the remains of partially-built resort structures.  Cracked tile floors split by root systems.  A pool filled with something that isn't water anymore.  The skeleton of floor-to-ceiling windows.  Perimeter crews have seen it.  Landmark: "don't push past the spa."

**The Watchtower:** Visible above the canopy from inside the compound: an unfinished vista tower.  Intended as a tourist lookout; a place for resort guests to ascend above the jungle canopy and take in the view.  Never completed, but the structure got tall enough to clear the treeline before construction stopped.  It's unreachable now (well inside forgespawn territory), but it's *there*, visible every day, a reminder of what this place was supposed to be.

It's become a morbid joke among the crews.  "How was night shift?" "Oh, you know... the watchtower's still standing."  Meaning: nothing's changed, we're still here, the jungle hasn't swallowed it yet.  The day it disappears behind the canopy, things have gotten measurably worse.

Potential future use: the upper floors above the canopy might serve as emergency refuge.  The forgespawn are jungle creatures; above the canopy might be above their territory.  Nobody's tested this theory and nobody's eager to.

These ruins are evidence of the timeline (it was safe enough to build here once) but nobody connects the dots publicly.

## Notes

- Named from the Jungle World sample names (variant of Roris).
- Scale reference: Avatar's Hell's Gate for the compound.  Snow Crash Raft / Johnny Mnemonic for stacked sea-can housing.
- The planet itself is genuinely beautiful and habitable.  The forgespawn infestation is concentrated around the vault/extraction site.  Elsewhere on Orris might still be the paradise it looks like from orbit.
- The forgespawn are ancient invaders who won their war against the Remnants and settled Orris long before humans arrived.  The vault breach re-concentrated them by restoring a stimulus they hadn't sensed in centuries.  Ceasing extraction would remove the stimulus and they'd disperse again over decades, but they'd still be here.  Stopping doesn't make Orris safe; it just makes it quieter.
