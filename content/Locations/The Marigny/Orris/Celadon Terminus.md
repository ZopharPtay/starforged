# Celadon Terminus

**Type:** Shuttle landing station / transit hub
**Planet:** [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Orris|Orris]]
**Region:** Outlands (The Marigny sector)

## Summary

The primary landing facility for public shuttle traffic between Paxton Orbital Station and the surface of Orris.  Think "downtown bus terminal."  One of the only places to land in this area; handles everything from budget commuter shuttles to private charters, though the tiers are physically separated.

## Layout

- **Landing pads:** Tiered by class.  Lower-tier (public) pads share the original main entrance.  Premium vessels got their own expansion over the years and handed down their old infrastructure.
- **Grand Atrium:** Main dome.  Giant display panels for pad assignments and departures.  Speakers droning status reports.  Crowded with People.
- **Locker bank:** Massive wall of storage lockers between the pads and the atrium.  The Cartel keeps several for dead drops.  High-traffic anonymity makes it ideal.
- **Cafshop:** Just beyond the lockers, facing the atrium.  Leight's usual turnaround point on past Cartel runs.
- **Front doors:** A bit fancy for doors.  The entryway has the worn feel of old money infrastructure that working-class traffic has ground down through volume.  Used to have separate entrances by tier; consolidation pushed everyone through the same doors.

## Transit Details

- Public shuttle: ~1 hour transit from Paxton.  Runs every ~2 hours.  Packed, uncomfortable, stripped bare.
- Better options exist: commuter shuttles (comfortable seats, fold-down desks), taxi-equivalent, private charter.  Leight rides the bus.
- Missing the last shuttle means staying overnight planetside.

## Cartel Presence

The Cartel uses locker dead drops here routinely.  Leight made countless runs to these lockers when he was working off his debt.  High foot traffic provides anonymity; the lockers are just lockers to everyone who isn't looking.

Complication (back pocket): a Cartel runner might recognize Leight hauling a tote through "their" terminal and assume he's running product for someone else.

## Notes

- Named from the Jungle World sample names list (Celadon = grey-green ceramic glaze).
- "Terminus" = end of the transit line.  Also: the farthest point Leight has ever gone on this planet until today.
