# The Cascade

**Type:** Mess hall / communal sanitation / central infrastructure
**Settlement:** [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Celadon/Celadon|Celadon]]
**Full Name:** The Cascade by Fontaine (ironic)

## Summary

The original infrastructure node the settlement calcified around.  Sheet-metal barn with raised slatted flooring, plastic furniture, strip lighting.  Named ironically after the celebrity chef restaurant the resort planned but never built.  Spraypainted signage.

Day-zero function that never changed: water, food, sanitation.  Everything else grew around it.

## Services

- **Mess kitchen:** Prepares ration packs in rotating variety.  Occasionally supplemented with fresh game or solar produce from rooftop gardens.
- **Communal hot showers:** Non-negotiable baseline.  The psychological transition between "on shift" and "off shift."  You walk in filthy; you walk out clean.  That's the reset.  Packed at shift change; quiet in between.  A social space in its own right.
- **Toilets:** Adjacent to showers, sharing the water supply.

## Roof Infrastructure

The Cascade has the largest uninterrupted roof surface in the compound.  It carries:

- **Solar collection:** Powers common areas, lighting, climate control.
- **Water collection/purification:** Purpose-built system maintained as shared infrastructure.  Feeds the kitchen, showers, and toilets.  The "official" water supply.
- **HVAC/dehumidification:** Climate control here isn't about heating; it's about drying the air enough that electronics don't corrode and food stores don't mold.  Vents on the roof.
- **Kitchen garden (small):** One corner, whatever's left after infrastructure claims its share.  Tended by kitchen staff.  Not a farm; enough for "tonight's special has fresh herb."

## Social Role

Socially central to the settlement.  Every path leads to or past it because that's where you eat, shower, and shit.  The hub of the hub-and-spoke.

The showers are where the transition from worker to person happens collectively.  The chatter there is different from the chatter in the bay or the mess.  Looser.  The day is over.

## Notes

- The raised slatted flooring is a retrofit (drainage in a rain-soaked environment).
- "The Cascade by Fontaine" references a celebrity chef restaurant the resort planned.  Nobody knows who Fontaine was.  The name stuck because it's funny.
