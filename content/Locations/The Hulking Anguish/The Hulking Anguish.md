# The Hulking Anguish - Physical Layout

![[Hulking Anguish Blueprint 1.png]]
## Overview

The SH-56V is a two-and-a-half deck vessel with a tapered wedge profile forward widening into a rectangular cargo body aft.  The bridge sits in a raised split-level nose.  Spider-leg segmented landing gear deploys from the belly, giving the ship significant ground clearance when landed.

**Scale:** Somewhere between the Razor Crest and Serenity.  Crew of three, designed for VIP courier operations on a freight chassis.  Every room exists for a reason.

**Deck heights:** ~10 ft per deck.  The cargo hold is double-height (~20 ft), with the mezzanine at upper deck level and the hold floor at mid deck level.

**Viewports:** None.  The hull is solid throughout.  All exterior views are provided by multipurpose display panels fed by external cameras. 

## Standard Furniture: The Bunk/Display/Table Unit

A standard SH-56 fitting found in every stateroom and the briefing room.  One piece of hardware, three configurations:

- **Display mode:** Unit flush against the wall; display face outward.  Functions as a wall screen.
- **Bed mode:** Hinges down from the wall on the long side; mattress face up, display face down.  Slightly elevated (chair serves as step up).
- **Table mode:** From bed position, rotates 180° on a pin so the display face is up.  Functions as a desk, drafting table, or dining surface.  Can be angled.

**Mechanism:** Gas struts on a wall rail (neutrally buoyant; push to desired height, stays put).  Manual friction latch locks height for flight/maneuvers.  No motors, no power dependency.  The hinge, pivot, and struts are purely mechanical.  Only the display itself requires power.

**Briefing room conversion:**  No additional work required, bunk is still there.

**Current state:** Most display panels are non-functional.  Oren replaced one (briefing room, for comms/news while relaxing).  The rest are dark until repaired or replaced.

## Upper Deck (Crew Country)

![[First/Locations/The Hulking Anguish/Upper Deck Closeup.png]]

Forward to aft:

### Bridge
Split-level, elevated in the ship's nose.  Over-pressurized atmosphere (Threshold's lung-capacity accommodation; ears pop when the hatch seals).  Pilot and co-pilot stations surrounded by display panels showing external camera feeds and ship systems.  Avionics/sensors suite in the nose dome.  Three hand-carved wooden knobs on cockpit switches (Oren's family tradition); the fourth switch is bare polymer.

### Airlock (Bridge)
Maintains the over-pressurized cabin (see above).  Internal pressure hatch between the flight deck and crew quarters.  The boundary between the bridge's elevated atmosphere and the rest of the ship.

### Staterooms
Three crew cabins in the SH-56V configuration (originally four in the SH-56 factory standard; one pair was converted to captain's quarters + briefing room).  Each cabin has a bunk/display/table unit.

- **SR1 (Starboard):** Captain's stateroom.  Same size as the other two.  Private connecting door to the briefing room.  Shares a cheater ensuite with the briefing room (de facto private since nobody sleeps in the briefing room).
- **SR2 (Port):** Crew cabin.  Shares a cheater ensuite with SR3.
- **SR3 (Port):** Crew cabin.  Shares a cheater ensuite with SR2.

The captain's only real privilege is not sharing a wall and having private access to the briefing room.

### Briefing Room (Starboard)
Adjacent to SR1 with a private connecting door.  Originally the fourth crew cabin; converted to a ready room in the SH-56V variant.

**Era notes:**
- Milspec: briefing table (display surface), chairs.  Captain enters privately; everyone else from the corridor.
- Trevane: Sova's private office/scheming room.
- Oren: workshop/office.  Build log, schematics spread on the display surface at drafting angle.  The most "him" room on the ship.
- Leight: undiscovered or recently discovered; Oren's workspace frozen in time.

**Future hook:** Removing the conference table and chairs converts this back to a cabin.  That's a "welcome to the family" gesture for a future crewmate.

### Galley
Central crew space.  Counter/cooktop on one side, communal dining table on the other.  Cold storage (cooler) adjacent on the port side.  This is where crew life happens: meals, conversation, planning.

### Airlock (Exterior)
Starboard side.  Primary crew EVA and docking access.  Station umbilical connects here.

### Ship's Locker
Aft starboard, adjacent to the exterior airlock.  Former crew gear storage and armoury in the milspec era.  Currently general secure storage.

### Cargo Hold Mezzanine
Aft section of the upper deck.  Open platform with railing (not bulkhead) overlooking the double-height cargo hold below.  The freight elevators (port and starboard side) connect the mezzanine to the mid deck cargo hold floor and the lower deck vehicle bay.

Access down to mid deck via interior ramp.  Pressure hatch at the top of the ramp (functional, milspec).  Empty arch at the bottom (Trevane-era decorative door removed by Oren).

## Mid Deck (Cargo + Engineering)

![[First/Locations/The Hulking Anguish/Mid Deck Closeup.png]]

### Luxury Suites (Port and Starboard)
Forward section, flanking the interior ramp.  Mirrored layouts.  Converted from cargo space during the Trevane era.  Interior walls (not bulkhead); interior doors (not pressure hatches).

Each contains:
- **Mudroom/entry** (exterior hatch on hull wall; for planetside excursions, not vacuum)
- **Living room** (central space; desk, bar, seating)
- **Dining area** (small table for two; intimate meals)
- **Aft vestibule** (junction: doors to living room, washroom, and cargo hold)
- **Washroom** (full: shower, toilet, sink, soaker tub, vanity/makeup desk; dead-end off vestibule)
- **Bedroom** (dead-end off living room; private retreat)

**Trevane-era guest flow:** Guests primarily boarded via the main cargo ramp into the hold (configured as lounge/dining/entertainment space), then were led to their suites.  The exterior suite hatches provided private access once settled.  Guests never needed to visit crew country.

**Current state:** Interior walls intact.  Plumbing likely functional.  Furnishings stripped or abandoned.  Bare deck with bolt holes, discolored patches, maybe a dangling wire.  Oren left these mostly alone; they weren't blocking his cargo rail restoration.

### Interior Ramp
Connects upper deck (crew country) to mid deck (suite/cargo hold level).  Pressure hatch at top; empty doorframe arch at bottom.

**Hidden compartment:** A classified-courier false panel is concealed in the ramp structure, disguised as a maintenance access panel.  Contains the Wraiths' asset network data chip (stashed by Commander Lantern during the boarding incident).  Undiscovered by any subsequent owner, including Leight.

### Hydroponic Garden / Greenwall
The aft face of the suite wall (the wall dividing the suites from the cargo hold), visible from the mezzanine and the cargo hold below.  Includes the arch above the former decorative door at the ramp base.  Originally installed by [[Mira Trevane]] as a lush greenwall; the visual centerpiece and first thing seen when entering via the main cargo ramp.  Served as the divider between "guest space" and "working space."

Converted to vegetables by [[Oren Herschel]].  Current state TBD (dead, overgrown, or stubbornly alive on automated irrigation).

### Cargo Hold
Double-height open space (20 ft, spanning mid deck to mezzanine level).  The working heart of the ship.  Aft cargo ramp drops down to ground level for loading.  Cargo rail system two-thirds rebuilt by Oren.  Side compartments from original milspec layout (former armoury alcoves; Oren restored as tool/project storage lockers).

**Freight elevators** (port and starboard side): mesh-style platform connecting lower deck vehicle bay → cargo hold floor → upper deck mezzanine.

### Fuel Tanks
Four tanks: two flanking the cargo hold, two flanking engineering.

### Engineering
Aft section.  Two maneuver thrusters (flanking, functional) and one E-drive (center-aft, dead).  The E-drive is the single point of failure keeping Leight in-system.  Accessible engineering space between the drives for maintenance.

## Lower Deck (Vehicle Bay)

![[First/Locations/The Hulking Anguish/Lower Deck Closeup.png]]

Belly-mounted bay beneath the forward portion of the ship (under the suites and forward ~2/3 of the cargo hold).  Does not extend beneath the aft cargo ramp section.

### Exterior Access Elevator
Large platform that lowers between the spider-leg landing gear.  Originally sized for two tactical vehicles side by side (SH-56 standard).  Milspec rapid deployment: elevator drops, vehicles roll off, elevator rises, ship lifts.

### Freight Elevators
Port and starboard side.  Mesh-style platform connecting vehicle bay → cargo hold → mezzanine (all three levels).  Internal circulation path; Leight can ride from the vehicle bay to crew-country level without using the ramp through the suites.

### Work Area
Space around the elevator platform.  Workbench/prep area with mismatched floor hardware (tie-down points from different eras: milspec tactical vehicle mounts, Trevane luggage racking anchors, Oren's project clamps).

**Current state:** Empty.  The hoverbike is stashed on Paxton (R3 dead zone), not yet aboard.  Oren's workbench may still have remnants of his tools.

## Circulation Summary

**Primary paths:**
- **Station boarding:** Exterior airlock (upper deck starboard) → galley/cabins
- **Cargo loading:** Aft cargo ramp → cargo hold → freight elevator up to mezzanine
- **Vehicle loading:** Exterior elevator (belly) lowers to ground → drive on → raise
- **Crew to hold:** Galley area → through pressure hatch → down interior ramp → cargo hold
- **Vertical shortcut:** Freight elevators connect all three decks (port and starboard)

**VIP path (Trevane era):**
- Main cargo ramp → hold (lounge/dining space) → led to suite
- Or: exterior suite hatch → mudroom → suite (once settled)
- Guests never needed to visit crew country
