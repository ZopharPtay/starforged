## Recap

[[Characters/Leight/Leight|Leight]] delivered Flint's late thruster assemblies to [[Characters/NPCs/Rowena|Rowena]], got paid in full (and in humiliation), composed himself at [[Celadon Terminus|the Cascade]], and is now walking through the rain toward the salvage yard on the south end of the compound.  He has about an hour before the next shuttle back to [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Paxton Orbital Station|Paxton]].

He has credits from Rowena's payment plus what [[Characters/NPCs/Flint|Flint]] fronted him; enough to cover the pickup now that she paid in full.

### Location

[[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Orris|Orris]], surface.  [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Celadon Terminus|Celadon Terminus]] compound, heading to [[Characters/NPCs/Marrow|Marrow]]'s salvage yard.

## Prompt

The salvage yard at the south edge of the compound.  Pick up Flint's parts from [[Characters/NPCs/Marrow|Marrow]], pay, and get back to the shuttle before the last run upwell.

Marrow is suspicious of strangers, but a small detail earns the transaction.  Leight doesn't know what he's picking up.  Just "a box of parts" and the credits to hand over.

## Play

There aren't any signposts leading to the salvage yard, but even in this weather it's easy to find.  He can spot the old shuttle wing suspended on posts above the yard from where he's standing.  He's going to have to walk back past Rowena's toward the outskirts.  He definitely didn't walk any faster in front of her shop.  Certainly not.

Marrow runs the salvage yard down here.   Whatever Rowena decides can't be fixed gets shunted off to him to tear down and salvage what he can; the parts go back to Rowena for repairs and round and round it goes.  Leight doesn't know what he's picking up, just "a box of parts" and the credits he's supposed to hand over in exchange.  A "no details, no questions" run like he's done all his life.  It didn't even occur to him to ask Flint for details.

When they said you can't miss the salvage yard, they meant it.  It's literally the last thing this side of the vines.  The yard itself is a large open space using the old post-mounted shuttle wing as the primary roof with tarps pulled tight in all directions from there, expanding coverage as time demanded.  The water pools on a couple of them but, in general, they are taut and angled well enough to serve their purpose.  There's a slight incline about a half-step up onto the split log flooring, the logs themselves scarred by dragging vehicles and heavy equipment across it and charred in places from the cutting torches over the years.  Some of the logs have clearly been replaced more recently than others.

Mud has been packed between the logs to keep it roughly level, but it does nothing at all for traction in his station boots.   He almost wipes on the damp logs a few times as he makes his way past a cluster of partially dismantled equipment toward the sound of work.  He can't wait to get off this miserable wet rock.  Some drifthead once tried to convince him that [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Celadon Terminus|Celadon]] was originally meant to be some fancy retreat for the gilt.  They burn cred to see the pretty colors it makes, but even _they_ wouldn't pay to visit this place.  Heck, Leight is just about ready to pay to _leave_.

The back of the yard where he can hear Marrow working is the driest, at least.  He comes around the bulk of an [[Lore/Equipment/Expedition Hover Barge|expedition skiff]] lying on its side and spots his man hunched over a makeshift workbench inspecting something.

Leight clears his throat and starts to introduce himself as he walks wide around Marrow to get inside his field of vision but not his personal space.

"You're in the wrong place."  Marrow hasn't bothered looking up.

Leight actually catches himself about to look around and check; with the trip he's had it wouldn't exactly surprise him.  "Flint sent me to pick up a few things, said you'd have it all packed and ready?" 

"Did he, now?" Marrow finally stands up to his full height and the cleats welded to his cybernetic leg drag across the floor as he turns to face Leight.  Not a threat, exactly, but he sure wants you to notice them.

"_They_ said you had something for _them_."  Leight emphasized the pronouns just a little.  With Marrow off to the side, Leight recognizes the battered thruster assembly he had been inspecting; a glance back at the skiff on its side confirms.  "Am I bringing another batch of thrusters back up?" he nods at the one on the bench.  "I just brought a case of them down to Rowena.  That one looks pretty rough."

Marrow relaxes slightly.  "I reckon maybe they did," his own slight emphasis acknowledging Leight's own.  "No, not thrusters this time.  At this rate we'll have a batch of those soon enough though.  These Mark 2s are garbage, look at this." He motions Leight's attention over to the overturned skiff.  Several of the thrusters on the port side have already been removed, a few others are clearly damaged; all told, almost a solid third on that side look like they'll need to be replaced.  "One idiot jumps off at the wrong time, the thing wobbles until it regains equilibrium, and smashes that side of the undercarriage against the rocks a dozen times in the process.  Nobody designed these things for this terrain.  What we need is - "  Leight knows this look.  He is in for a lecture from a man who is passionate about a topic he himself could not care less about.  He's already tuning it out.  He doesn't have time for this if he is going to make the shuttle back up the well tonight.  

After a few minutes of nods and non-committal affirmations he finds an opening.  "But you have enough for now, right?  So what am I bringing back upwell to Flint today?"  Marrow isn't exactly surprised at someone not wanting to hear his diatribe on the poor design and the improvements he wants to make.  He's used to it.  Not that he has ever let that stop him from bringing it up.  Repeatedly.

"Right.  Let's get you packed, lug that tote over to the bench and I'll load you up."  

Marrow pulls a sled - very much like the one Leight just left at Rowena's - out of the warehouse with a pile of parts on it, his cleated "feet" never once losing purchase on the logs.  The sound they make would be terrifying in the dark, Leight muses, as the sled drifts to a stop beside him.

He points parts out as he reads a list off his terminal:  "three actuator servos, one bank of injector nozzles, and ... Flint's special order."  The "special order" is a couple of sealed, pristine white boxes.  Those go in first.  They are "nervous around strangers", Marrow informs Leight with a weighted look before he drapes a stained old cloth over them.  Then he gently piles the remaining greasy and damaged parts on top.  The injector bank alone gets a slight grunt of effort and fills most of the remaining space.  

"You know, these injectors on the Mark Two -"

"Credits," Leight interrupts, perhaps a bit too excitedly, looking at the time on the terminal he offers to Marrow.  "Go ahead and transfer it over and I'll be out of your hair.  I need to get on that last shuttle."  Marrow taps his own terminal and the funds transfer, his mechanic's monologue derailed again.

Leight grabs the tote and, between the weight and his traction on the logs, he can barely carry it over to the sled.  He sets it down and grabs the control bar.  Marrow just smiles in feigned chagrin.  "Those cost extra."

It's a very long and very slow walk over the wet logs and through the street back to the Terminus with enough breaks that he wonders if maybe he should have thought of some way to talk Marrow into walking his sled with him, even if it meant listening to the guy's ramblings.  

Boarding has already started when he finally gets to the landing pad, completely soaked through again, and worn out from the effort.  One last look out the Terminus window before boarding and he can see the ring of fire in the distance as the boundary crews start their daily shift change from daytime machetes to the evening torchers.  They say the vines grow faster at night and the machetes can't keep up.  They say the fire helps keep whatever else is out there away at night.  They say a lot of things.  It can't all be true.  But what IS true is how comfortable the lid of that old polymer tote is going to be once he gets it settled and can sit the rest of the trip home.  He's definitely grabbing one of the sleds at the landing pad when he gets there.
