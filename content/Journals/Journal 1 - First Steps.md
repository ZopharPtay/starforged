## Recap

  [[Nassir (Leight) Okoro|Nassir "Leight" Okoro]] just bought [[First/Characters/The Hulking Anguish/The Hulking Anguish|The Hulking Anguish]] at auction on [[Paxton Orbital Station]].  The ship flies sublight but the e-drive is dead.  He's broke, free from [[The Cartel]] (technically), and stuck in their territory until that drive fires up.

  He's making a short hop to [[Flint]]'s shop to get the e-drive assessed.  Flint is an independent mechanic, former Cartel asset who got burned.  They have that in common, at least.

### Location: [[The Marigny]] (Outlands)

  **Paxton Orbital Station** - orbits a jungle world.  Thousands.  The Cartel runs things.  Cheap diversions, ineffectual official authority.

## Prompt

  The Hulking Anguish's spider-leg landing gear unfolds onto Flint's service platform, the hull ticking as it cools.  Nassir is about to walk into a conversation where he needs a favor he can't pay for.

## Play

The shuttle from the planet to the Paxton Auction House had been a rough flight, but at least he hadn't had to worry about whether the commercial hopper would hold air.  This was ridiculous.  Leight couldn't help wonder if he had made a terrible mistake buying this ship, and could see why nobody was bothering to outbid him.  He bought himself a genuine death trap.  He knew that patch in the hull was proof of exactly that, but actually leaving the landing pad in it sure had a way of bringing it to the front of his mind.  They certainly got the _Anguish_ part of the name right.

He had this next part planned, at least.  Alright "plan" is a strong word for "I'll see if I can convince Flint to help", but it was _something_.  Leight didn't have anything of value to trade for the work he knew he needed, but hopefully Flint was willing to wheel a bit.  While they used to be friendly, they weren't ever really _friends_; he was pretty sure they were at the very least "temporarily mutually beneficial".  He laughed a bit when his brain surfaced the old "it's complicated" relationship status.  That it is.  Complicated.  

"We'll need to play this live", he told nobody in particular.  "See how Flint reacts when they see me.  Then we can decide if we're asking a favor for future payment, or offering services in barter."   He's assuming it'll be the latter; nobody extends credit to someone like him, but who knows.  Maybe Flint's condition isn't a whole lot more flexible given their recent "working vacation" in the labour sectors when they got pinched.

He sets the _Anguish_ down on the public pads off Ring 5, keying in the permit code indicating he's there visiting a local shop on business.  That gives him a couple hours before the dock fees start racking up.  He spends most of the tube ride from the outer pads to the Ring 5 proper going over the various scenarios;  if Flint is happy to see him - yeah, nevermind, unlikely.  If Flint is too busy to see him today, where can he stay tonight?  He can't afford a room, and he _really_ doesn't want to sleep in stationary orbit trusting that can to hold air overnight.  Certainly can't afford to dock overnight.  Never any good options.  If he can get Flint to at least flag the _Anguish_ as a visitor of his shop for the night, he can sleep off the night docked.  That's the absolute minimum he needs to get out of this.  With any luck he can walk away with a job that won't put too much strain on the ship and trade for a full system diagnostic after the work is done.  Diagnostic in advance is obviously the ideal, but Nassir had never been an optimist,. and Leight had seen so much more since those days.

He nearly missed his stop running through the different plays, but did get off at R5-14.  Those with a few cred usually took one of the waiting pedicabs from here, but it wasn't a long walk and he didn't have it to spare anyway.  This whose section of R5 was old large pods forming the structure of the station itself.  Other rings and floors have subdivided the pods, converted a pod into multiple residences or shops, divided one pod into multiple levels... down here, a pod is a pod.  They are great for large shops and warehouses that don't want or need the luxuries of clean lines or smooth walls.

R5-14-23.  Flint's shop.  It looks exactly like Leight expected; like not a damned thing.  A giant open container with a man-high wall blocking off the lower portion for crimes of convenience and prying eyes.  The light over the door is green, the only indication there is a shop there or that it is open for business.  Flickering lights seen over the wall looks like Flint is "home" and welding something.   At least that means there won't be any witnesses if he gets humiliated.   He remembers the old adage that you should never arrive empty-handed and mentally boots himself for not thinking to grab a couple cans of something to share.  He could head back to the tube.... but he wasn't fooling himself, he's just procrastinating at that point.  Besides; he likely didn't have the parking allowance left to backtrack to a shop, get back here, and then have the discussion he had to have.

He hits the access panel beside the door and it slides open; the graffiti-style lion a touch foreboding as he steps into the lion's den, hat (figuratively at least) in hand.

Once inside the shop, it's absolutely packed.  Flint seems to have projects piled on top of projects and a whiteboard off to the side looks like they were trying to keep track of them all and gave up.  Each of the four repair bays has a vehicle in it; one is lifted up and has a vehicle _under_ it with a diagnostic rig happily flashing and beeping away trying to troubleshoot whatever is wrong with it.  Crates of parts line one wall.  If there is an organization to them, only Flint might know it.  Three pallets with boxes seem to have a blanket thrown on top forming a small nest.  Is Flint sleeping here?  

And then there's Flint.  Hunched over a thruster assembly, welding visor down, clearly too caught up in the work to notice a new person walking in.  "Distracted" was not how Leight would describe Flint.  The little mechanic wasn't exactly jumpy, but they rarely let people sneak up on them.  An empty water jug and a stack of empty food wrappers nearby hint that they may not have even taken time to eat a proper meal recently.  The near-empty wine bottle on the desk doesn't strike Leigth as Flint's style, but hey... hard times, right?  Come to think of it, that handwriting on the project board isn't all that consistent either.  Are they sharing the shop? 

Leight grabs the mug off what would theoretically be a receptionist's desk if there was one and fills it from from the little washroom off to the side before intentionally and noisily making his way over towards the mechanic.  No point scaring them if they are too focused to notice him.  He can almost place a hand on Flint's shoulder before the sparks die down enough that Flint sees a shadow and looks up.

Flint covers it up quickly, but Leight can tell he isn't who Flint expected to be right behind them.  He holds his arms out in a universal "I come in peace" gesture, and slowly offers the water with a sheepish grin.   Flint looks at the water, confused at first before glancing down at his empty jug and taking it.  

"I told you guys", Flint begins.   "Look, I'm out", Leight says.  It's as if they were speaking the same thought.  Both somewhat defensive, neither really sure where they stand with the other with the foundation of their previous working relationship dismantled on both sides.

"I don't care what you're out of, I'm done with The Cartel.  I did my time and kept my peace, I don't owe you shit, we're done.  And look where the hell _that_ got me."

It must be the stress, Leight tells himself.   Flint doesn't swear, as a rule.

"I know 'you two never got along' is underselling things, but you knew [[Vuldar Wolfe |Warden]].  He was a thug, but he was good to me.  Even if he wasn't good to you.  He got me out.  Like out, out."

"Knew?"

"Knew.  All I have right now is the rust bucket that got me here.  And maybe a mechanic?  I need work.  Doesn't have to be _honest_ work, but work."

Flint glances toward the door and back.  Apprehension, maybe guilt?  "It's never easy, is it?"  They could have been talking about any number of things; Leight wisely let it mean all of them and didn't reply directly.   Instead, gesturing toward the empty water jug silently offering to refill while they talk.  Hoping the tension could finally break with a kind gesture after a moment of vulnerability.   It wasn't calculated, exactly, but Leight wasn't exactly naive either.

"Look", Flint starts when Leight gets back, "let's grab a drink tonight.  I need to get this thruster bearing done before the end of the day, I'm already late; it was promised yesterday.  Let's talk later, man.  First drink is on you, though.  Meet me at the Gentleman Loser, some time around 18?"

Leight hopes he suppressed the flinch at the perfectly normal "first round is on you" comment.  He definitely doesn't have money for a night out.  "Yeah.  Yeah, alright, but I can't stay long, yah.  One or two tops, but let's catch up.  And, uh... since I'm buying the first round, can you drop my registration in the system?  The less I pay for the dock fees, the more I can spend on your tab."  He jokes, hoping it sounds convincing, knowing it's not at all a joke.  Maybe there's something in the hold worth bartering for a couple drinks.  Dockmasters aren't keen on barter, but most shops on Paxton aren't so proud.
