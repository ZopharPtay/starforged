## Recap

[[Characters/Leight/Leight|Leight]] just left [[Characters/NPCs/Flint|Flint]]'s shop.  The conversation went better than he feared; not great, but not a door in the face either.  Flint kicked him loose with a "meet me at the Gentleman Loser around 18:00" and went back to the thruster bearing that was already a day late.

It's mid-afternoon.  He has a few hours to kill on a station he's lived on his entire life, with nothing to do and nowhere to be until drinks.  No shift at the Terminus today.  No crates to load.  Just time, which he hasn't had the luxury of in a while.

### Location

[[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Paxton Orbital Station|Paxton Orbital Station]] in [[Locations/The Marigny/The Marigny|The Marigny]] sector.  Orbits [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Orris|Orris]], a jungle world.

## Prompt

Mid-afternoon on Paxton with nowhere to be until 18:00.  For the first time in months, Leight is walking these corridors without a destination.  The reader's first real look at the station through his eyes when he isn't panicking about money or rehearsing a pitch.

## Play

Two months solid grinding shifts, commuting downwell to sling freight.  Leight can barely remember the last time he had an evening where he didn't shamble from the shuttle to his hab and pass out just to catch the first one down in the morning to line up for a wage slot.  One advantage of wage slots down well is everyone has to take the shuttle down, so he's always reasonably sure that as long as he can get on the first one out, he can get a slot for the day.  You get some shifts up here and folks line up the night before and sleep in the corridors to lock in their slot the next day.  It's inhuman.  It's life on Paxton as drift.  

He figured he'd never have a shot at the downwell slots at all if he hadn't made some contacts in his time down there with [[The Cartel]], and if someone in The Cartel didn't enjoy using someone on the outs as an example of "life could always be worse".  But he had A Plan.  Sure, sure, everyone has A Plan, but _his_ is going to work.  Daily shifts for the months leading up to the auction had been just enough to get the [[Characters/The Hulking Anguish/The Hulking Anguish|Anguish]].  And now he has a mechanic!  Maybe.  Hopefully?

He's already on the tube to R3 when he realizes he's been heading home on autopilot.  He can't space out now, he has _work_ to do.  He makes eye contact with the passenger next to him and introduces himself:  "Leight.   Captain of the Malcontent Marsupial.  Sup."  The stranger gives a noncommittal grunt in reply and just happened to end up a step or two further away after the tube jostled.  They're always doing that.  She definitely wasn't stepping back from the drifthead.  It's a cool name for a ship, she just didn't "get it", and if he's going to be making a name for himself as a courier, it's going to have to be in a ship people "get".

The auction house paperwork has it registered under the previous owner's name: The Hulking Anguish.  What a stupid name.  Who is trusting a hulking _anything_ to transport their cargo quickly?  Quick, reliable, uncatchable.  

She gets off at the next stop - coincidence, surely - and he spots another test subject running for the tube.  Leight holds the door for the old man, who nods his thanks.  "No problem.   Leight, captain of the Asynchronous Anomaly, always happy to help."  That one's better.  No reaction from the old man, but it's a better name.  Asynchronous Anomaly.  That's a fast and unpredictable ship, right there.  He pulls out his hand term and starts playing with typefaces superimposed over the hull.   That's a LONG name.  And what's the mascot for that?  Everyone knows the Obsidian Fracture from the trids; you remember the words on the hull, the image of the shattered black skull.  _THAT'S_ a ship name. 

He gets off at his stop on R3 and heads starward toward his hab.  Oh!  Is this his last night in his hab?   He has a ship!   Can he sleep in the ship while it's in a service cradle?  They'd never know, right?  That thought distracts him the whole way back to his (OLD!) place.  He grabs a quick shower and a  fresh skinsuit, his brain flitting between thinking about moving onto the Flickering Vagrant - nah, rubbish - and more names.  

He needs totes before he can pack, and he's too excited to sit still.   He goes for a wander back planetward, towards the Promenade where he knows folks will be out shopping.  He's introducing himself every few minutes.  Captain of the Bombastic Perdition.  Of the Illustrious Sanctity.  

The children running around the Prom are far more interested in the jin flitting across his knuckles than in hearing about Voidcrawler Nine or The Penultimate Sin.  

He was Captain of the Reluctant Scourge at the shrine when he dipped a fingertip into the oil and touched a small dab on his inner wrist and muttered "for luck" to himself.

Captain Leight of the Anguished Forgespawn thanked the lady at the water station and assured her she'd be hearing from Okoro Logistics again soon.

"... whatever, man.  You want fries or not?", the kid asked the captain who embarrassingly tripped over the name Inoperable-No-Wait-Inexorable Per-Per-Perdition!

The Darkblade Eternal's cheerful captain welcomed fellow passengers on to the tube back down to R5.

Captain Leight Okoro of the freighter Grim Overture and his twin, Leight Okoro - owner and operator of Okoronaughtics Enterprises Unlimited  - both made an appearance outside of [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Far Provenance|Far Provenance]]'s shuttered windows while peering in at what appeared to be an old mask of a ghost?  A ghost... a wraith?  The Wraith's Countenance?  The Vengeful Wraith?  Wraithspawn?  Unrepentant Wraith?  

18:10 rolls around and he still hasn't settled on a name as he steps into [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/The Gentleman Loser|The Gentleman Loser]].  See... The Gentleman Loser.  _That's_ a name, right there.  The Gentleman Wraith?  He takes a deep breath as the door slides open, and he suddenly deflates back down to just Leight Okoro, The Man Begging For A Mechanic again as he steps over the threshold.
