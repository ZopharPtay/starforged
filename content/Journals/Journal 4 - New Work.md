The deal is done.  [[Characters/NPCs/Flint|Flint]] agreed to barter: ship repairs in exchange for gopher work, on their schedule.  The evening at [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/The Gentleman Loser|The Gentleman Loser]] ended warm, almost friendly.  Leight slept aboard [[Characters/The Hulking Anguish/The Hulking Anguish|The Hulking Anguish]], still docked in Flint's service cradle.

### Location

[[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Paxton Orbital Station|Paxton Orbital Station]] in [[Locations/The Marigny/The Marigny|The Marigny]] sector.  Departing via commercial shuttle to the jungle world below.

## Prompt

First task on Flint's list: deliver a set of repaired thruster assemblies to a client planetside and collect the balance owed.  Then use that money to pick up a part from a supplier downworld and bring it back.

What Flint doesn't mention: the thrusters are late.  The client is going to dispute the price.  Leight is walking into a collection problem with no backup and no authority.  Whatever he collects funds the second half of the errand.  If he comes up short, he figures it out or comes home empty-handed.

Flint transfers 1,000 credits to cover shuttle fare and the pickup.  The part costs 2,000.  Neither of them mentions the gap.

Possible approaches:
- Play the professional courier: deliver, present the invoice, stay calm and firm
- Lean on the Cartel-adjacent reputation (his or Flint's) to pressure payment
- Go in soft, read the situation, and adapt based on what the client gives him
- Accept partial payment gracefully and work the shortfall at the supplier's end instead

## Play

It doesn't take long for Flint to find a use for him.  [[Leight]] wakes up the next morning to the slowly pulsing light on the bulkhead console indicating a new message in the comm buffer.  He pulls it up to see that Flint has sent him some instructions.   Caffeine first, reading comes after the eyes open.

He makes his way from the bunk aboard [[Characters/The Hulking Anguish/The Hulking Anguish|the Anguish]], through his morning routine, landing in the galley with a mug of steam clearing his sinuses.  It's a cheap herbal blend with a synthetic caff pill dissolved in it, but it does the trick most mornings and he's come to appreciate how the slight bite of the dried ginger hides the caff pill's taste.

Food is tight after splurging on the drink last night, but business is business.  He tells himself it wasn't a waste, it was an _investment_, as he dips into the ship's emergency rations for one of the flavorless survival nutripacks.  It's a bad habit, especially in a ship that may not make it to the next station, but you can't Shade a growling stomach.

By the time the pill kicks in, he's ready for that message.  The hand terminal has seen better days, but what around here hasn't.  He has to thumb the lug a second time before it wakes up and signs him into the shipboard systems.  The default landing page flickers on occasion, and he would have to drag the display a bit to read the news ticker around the crack in the screen if he cared enough to bother reading it.  Thankfully the net handshake is still working fine; the Anguish syncs comm buffers while he takes another sip.  Then another.  Annnd another.  The station-default 360° image of Paxton turns lazily on the display with the green planet off in the corner while he waits.

The message comes through just as he's ready to give up and just take the message at the wall console.  Sometimes "no tech" is better than "old tech".  With how long that took to load he's almost afraid to even try to open it without another pot of the tea, but it opens well enough now that it's cached locally on the term.

Looks like Flint needs him to make a run down the well.  Drop some finished parts off, collect the balance due, and buy some supplies to bring back.  He can't take the Anguish in its current condition, so he'll be on the commercial shuttle.  He wishes he had checked this message a bit earlier, there's no way he'll make the next departure so he's stuck waiting a few hours.  Depending on how the trip goes, he might not be making it back to Paxton tonight at all at this rate.  He rinses out the mug and packs a small overnight bag before making his way to Flint's to grab the package.

Making his way from the service cradle back through R5 in the morning is a different experience.  He dips [[the shrine]] on the way through, for luck, and notes that [[Far Provenance]] is still locked tight and dark.  They weren't open yesterday evening when he was making his way to [[The Gentleman Loser]] either; {possibly clean that up once [[Journal 2 - Placeholder]] is fleshed out to reduce redundancy} the shop must keep odd hours.  He idly wonders if it's part of the "mysterious" gimmick to get people to wonder what's inside or if the proprietor just isn't currently station-side.  

A brief pause outside Flint's to compose himself before strutting inside with more bravado than he feels.  There's a tote on the reception desk containing a comm dot and an awkwardly-shaped box.  The doors to the workspace are closed and quiet, so he taps his term to the dot to pull the message off and helps himself to a refill of his canteen while he waits for the sync and decode.

It's mostly the same message from earlier with a few new details; credits have been transferred to his terminal to cover the shuttle fare and the balance for the pickup.  Oh, by the way, the delivery was promised last week.  He drops the awkward box on one of the sleds, taps his terminal to the control pad to transfer the access token and the lift drive buzzes to life.  The next scheduled shuttle departure isn't for a few hours; it will take half that to get there at the pace the sleds move, but sitting here twiddling his thumbs won't accomplish anything.  If he misses this flight he's off-station overnight for sure, early is better than late.
