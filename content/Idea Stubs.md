## Locations

### The Condensation Shrine

**Oracle roll:** Descriptor 59 (Mysterious) + Focus 65 (Resource)

A place on a station or settlement where water condenses in ways engineering can't explain.  People collect it with bottles.  A little cult of regulars.

- Why hasn't it been monetized?  Best answer: someone tried, and it stopped.  Moved.  Twice.  Now people are superstitious.  Even the local power structure won't touch it.
- Alternative: the water isn't normal.  Tastes too good.  People who drink it report strange dreams.  Whoever tested it walked away spooked.
- Best fit: a station near a ghost light (white dwarf), or a location with precursor tech underneath.  Ties to Horrors or Precursors truth.
- Not Paxton (wrong vibe; too commercial a station for something unmoneyed to survive without a supernatural reason, and we haven't established a ghost light nearby).

## Characters

### Bad Prosthetic Eye

A character (not Marrow) with a single bad cybernetic eye.  Company-issued replacement that was never calibrated right; gives them headaches.  Either removed it (eye patch / empty socket) or disabled it (dead glass eye that doesn't track).  The kind of detail that defines a character when it's their *only* visible prosthetic rather than one item on a list.

### Celadon Terminus: Return Visit Details

On a future Orris visit, Leight notices compound improvements that weren't there before:
- An awning over the Cascade doorway made from Bulldog hull plating (still stenciled "BULLDOG"; nobody bothered to paint over it).
