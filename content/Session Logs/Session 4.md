# Session 4

## Summary

A long worldbuilding and infrastructure session.  No narrative play advanced, but significant campaign foundation work was done: a new NPC and location generated via oracles, the Quartz publishing pipeline established, and deep lore developed for the paragon/magic system.

## Key Decisions

- **Far Provenance** created as a spiritual antiquarian shop on Paxton Station, proprietor Ike Sutton ("Grudge" among couriers/fixers).  Historian turned criminal fence, seeking specific artifacts for an unnamed organization.
- **Paragons lore** locked in: powers fade when leaving compatible planetary systems.  Multiple compatible planets exist per mutation type but are rare and not common knowledge.  The fade gutters unpredictably rather than declining cleanly.  Possible connection to precursor-era contamination.
- **Leight is unaware** his power will fade if he leaves the system.  This will become a critical plot point on his first out-of-system run.
- **"Protect the Secret" vow** clarified: the secret is the locations of compatible planets.
- **10-11 possible paragon types** identified (Shade, Kinetic, Firebrand, Empath, Seer, Looper, Healer, Naturalist, Infiltrator, Haunted, plus Lore Hunter as a stretch).
- **The Hulking Anguish's armament** is unestablished despite the Starship asset saying "armed."  Added to TODO.
- **Quartz site** live at https://zopharptay.github.io/starforged/ with catthode theme.
