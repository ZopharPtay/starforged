# Session 14

## Summary

Short planning/design session.  No prose, no gameplay.  Focused on the Hulking Anguish's interior layout and scale, the vehicle bay design, and resolving the hoverbike's current location.  Established the Journal 3.5 interstitial format for future vignettes.

## Key Decisions

### Ship Scale and Layout
- **Dropped the C-130 comparison.**  A C-130 cargo hold is ~410 sq ft; far too small for what the Anguish needs to contain.  The ship is now described as smaller than a Firefly-class (crew of 3 vs 9, no shuttlecraft) but substantially larger than a single-deck vessel.
- **Two decks:** main deck (bridge, living spaces, cargo hold) and a lower belly (vehicle bay).
- **Four cabins** (symmetry over strict crew-of-three logic).
- **Cargo hold is partially segmented:** large main area with side bays/compartments that have been repurposed through eras (armoury → luxury storage → Oren's tool lockers).

### Vehicle Bay Design
- Belly-mounted, accessed externally via an elevator platform that lowers between the spider-leg landing gear.
- Internal access from main deck via hatch/ladder.
- **Milspec (SH-56 standard):** Sized for two tactical vehicles side by side.  Workbench/prep space in the bay proper.
- **VIP era:** One side still a vehicle bay (dignitary transport); other side reconfigured for luggage racking and toy storage (jet-ski equivalent cradles, etc.).
- **Oren era:** Luxury racking stripped, workbench restored, project storage.
- **Leight's era:** Empty.  Mismatched floor hardware, workbench with possible remnants of Oren's tools.

### Hoverbike Status
- The bike is NOT aboard the Anguish.  It's stashed in the R3 dead zone on Paxton; chained under a tarp in a shuttered commercial area.
- Gets moved aboard later (potential future scene/beat).
- Backstory deliberately left open (station moped, Cartel work tool, racing circuit, earned during dock shifts; TBD).

### Journal Format
- Interstitial vignettes use decimal numbering: Journal 3.5, 4.5, etc.
- Full chapters keep integer numbers.
- If further subdivision is ever needed, halve again (3.25 between 3 and 3.5).
- Quartz sidebar sort is alphabetical by default; solvable via `sortFn` override if it becomes an issue.  Not implementing now (YAGNI).

### Starforged Mechanics Confirmed
- No cargo capacity mechanic exists.  Ship modules are pure XP spend with no slot limit or space budget.
- Future additions (medbay, etc.) are fiction-first with no mechanical tradeoff against cargo.
