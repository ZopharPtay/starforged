### Key Decisions

#### Ship Layout (finalized)
- **Upper Deck:** Bridge (split-level nose, all-displays, no viewports), bridge airlock, three staterooms (SR1 captain starboard, SR2/SR3 crew port), briefing room (starboard, private door from SR1), galley (central), exterior airlock (starboard), ship's locker, cargo hold mezzanine (railing, not bulkhead).
- **Mid Deck:** Two luxury suites (port/starboard, mirrored, flanking interior ramp), cargo hold (double-height, aft ramp), engineering (two thrusters flanking, one E-drive center), four fuel tanks.  Freight elevators port and starboard.
- **Lower Deck:** Vehicle bay (belly-mounted, exterior access elevator, freight elevators port/starboard, workbench area).

#### Drive Layout
- One E-drive (FTL, central, dead).  Two maneuver thrusters (sublight, flanking, working).  Redundancy on the constantly-used drives.

#### Bunk/Display/Table Unit
- Standard SH-56 fitting.  Three modes: display (flush wall screen), bed (hinges down), table (rotates 180° on pin, display face up).
- Gas struts on wall rail for height adjustment.  Manual friction latch.  No power dependency for the mechanical functions.

#### False Panel / Data Chip Relocation
- False panel moved from "secure hold" to cargo hold ramp (disguised as maintenance access panel).
- Data chip placed inside the false panel compartment.
- Both are undiscovered by Leight (corrected from earlier lore that said he knew about the panel).

#### Viewports
- None anywhere on the ship.  Hull is solid throughout.  All views via display panels + external cameras.

#### Luxury Suites
- Interior walls (not bulkhead), interior doors (not pressure hatches).
- Each has: mudroom (exterior hatch, planetside only), living room, dining for two, bedroom (dead-end off living room), aft vestibule (junction), full washroom with soaker tub.
- Trevane-era guest flow was via main cargo ramp into the hold (resort lobby experience), not through crew country.
- Exterior suite hatches are convenience access once settled, not the grand entrance.

#### Hydroponic Garden
- Located on the aft face of the suite wall, visible from cargo hold and mezzanine.
- Includes arch above former decorative door at ramp base.
- Mira's centerpiece; first thing seen entering via cargo ramp.
- Oren converted to vegetables.  Current state TBD.

#### Ramp Details
- Pressure hatch at top (milspec, functional).
- Empty arch at bottom (Trevane decorative door removed by Oren).

#### Other
- No viewports in staterooms (submarine philosophy).
- Cheater ensuites: captain/briefing share one; two crew cabins share one.
- Captain's "privilege" is just a connecting door to the briefing room; same cabin size.
- Ship's locker is former armoury; not the location of any secrets.
