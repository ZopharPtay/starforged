# Session 8

## Summary

Worldbuilding and prose session.  Named the jungle world (Orris) and the shuttle landing station (Celadon Terminus).  Established the public shuttle as a city-bus equivalent: packed, uncomfortable, ~1 hour transit, runs every 2 hours.  Developed Leight's black iron token as a precursor wheel bearing he fidgets with constantly (knuckle walks, roller thumbing, finger spins); he doesn't know what it is.  Coined "jin" (from djinn) as Forge slang for a lucky charm and "drift" as salvage/flotsam.  Established Leight's history with Celadon Terminus: many Cartel dead-drop runs but never left the building.

Wrote Journal 5 prose covering: the wait at the shuttle port, the transit down, arrival at Celadon Terminus, the walk through the Grand Atrium past the locker bank and cafshop, and through the front doors for the first time.  Ended at the threshold of the outside world.

## Key Decisions

- Planet named Orris (from Jungle World sample names, variant of Roris).
- Landing station named Celadon Terminus.
- Economy deliberately left vague; no specific credit amounts in fiction.
- Leight's iron is a black iron wheel bearing (~1.5", domed, exposed rollers, two missing = lanyard hole).  He doesn't know its nature.
- "Jin" = lucky charm (Forge slang).  "Drift" = salvage/flotsam.  "Drift shop" = junk store.
- Leight has been to Celadon Terminus before (Cartel dead drops) but never left the building.
- Shuttle transit is the city bus; other tiers exist (commuter, taxi, charter) but Leight rides the bus.
- The Cartel dead-drop lockers at Celadon Terminus are a stored complication (someone might recognize Leight).
