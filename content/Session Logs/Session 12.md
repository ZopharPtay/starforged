# Session 12

## Summary

Off-the-books brainstorming session.  No prose, no gameplay.  Two major topics: the modular container system used across the Forge (and specifically at Celadon), and the general structure of Paxton Orbital Station.

## Key Decisions

### Forge Modular Container System

- **PIP (Pearson Industrial Platform):** Cubical structural frame, 12 ft per edge.  The base manufacturing unit.  Nonferrous polyalloy (iron is rare/culturally significant precisely because everyday construction doesn't use it).
- **Corvin HexClad:** Stamped hexagonal-relief panels bolted to PIP exteriors.  Originally a trademarked product; now genericized.  Knockoffs everywhere.
- **Sealant:** Spray-on elastomeric coating (~5mm, bed-liner texture) for pressure membrane in vacuum.  Degrades visibly over time.
- **Alloy degradation:** Oxidizes into distinctive bluish dust.  Water carves pale streaks.  Bio-fouling layers on top in humid environments.  Stress cracks propagate from fastener points.
- **Configurations:** Pip (1 cube), Pea (2×1×1, shipping), Slab (2×2×1, habitation), Pod (2×2×2, station architecture), Mod (4×4×4, station structural unit).
- **Terminology:** pip, pea, slab, pod, mod.  "Corvin" or "hex clad" for panels.  "Solar" for food grown under a real star.

### Celadon Settlement

- Restructured from one monolithic note into a directory: Celadon.md (settlement overview), Celadon Terminus.md (building), The Racks.md, Rowenas Bay.md, The Plaza.md, The Cascade.md.
- The 'racks: stacked slabs (3 interior pips + 1 balcony pip), three high.  Punched corners, staggered rotation, favela energy.  Bridges ranging from official gangways to single planks.
- Rowena's bay: PIPs as structural walls, U-shaped profile, ribbon bay doors (pip-wide hex-clad panels on roller spindles), rooftop social space with garden, grill, and secret soaker tub.
- Water: decentralized.  Each structure catches/processes its own.  The Cascade is the communal hub (hot showers, mess, toilets).
- Food: ration packs (baseline), game (middle tier), solar produce (luxury/barter currency).
- Fire: no fear outdoors (the jungle is soaking wet; they burn the perimeter nightly).  Discipline only inside hab cans.
- Settlement origin: military-camp layout that calcified.  Tents → peas → slabs → stacked.
- Spin launcher replaced mass driver (centrifugal, no ferrous requirement).
- Normal fauna exists alongside forgespawn; legitimate hunting parties provide cover for Cartel narcotic crews.

### Paxton Orbital Station

- **Origin:** Built by a Founder Clan as back-of-house for a multi-resort plan (4-6 resorts).  Now extraction support; Cartel governs day-to-day.
- **Structure:** Vertical spine, five rings on upper half (diamond profile).  Lower half has conical fairing over unbuilt R6-R8 anchors.  Gold-tinted high-albedo hull cladding.
- **Rings:** R1 (Cartel executive), R2 (residential), R3 (docking/commercial, dead zones), R4 (industrial), R5 (industrial, Flint's shop).
- **Gravity:** Artificial (grav generators; same tech as hover vehicles).  Rings don't spin.  Can fail.
- **Orbit:** Geostationary above Celadon.  ~1 hour shuttle transit.
- **Core/spine:** Reactors, life support, grav generators, transit tube (low-g), freight elevator, coolant/radiators.  Non-public.
- **Docking:** R3 primary (Cartel chokepoint, most airlocks inactive).  R4/R5 loading pads (short-term, shop-vouched).  Service cradles (long-term, shop lease).
- **Shuttle:** Bus with stops.  R3 (formal customs), R5 (worker stop, spot checks), R1 (on-demand).
- **Addressing:** R{ring}{deck}-{section}-{unit}.  Deck A implied/dropped.
- **Orientation:** Fixed (no spin).  Planet-ward vs star-ward sides; gradient preference, not hard divide.
- **R3 commercial:** Originally mix of company essentials + leased independents.  Now resident-owned + unkillable franchise cockroaches.
- **Visibility from surface:** Fixed warm-gold point of light in night sky.
