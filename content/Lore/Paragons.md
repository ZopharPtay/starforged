# Paragons

Rare individuals who wield supernatural abilities tied to specific planets within the Forge.  Not magic in the traditional sense; evolutionary mutations granted by the strange energies of certain worlds.

## The Nature of the Gift

Some planets in the Forge inexplicably grant mutations to those who settle there.  These mutations manifest as abilities that defy conventional science: telekinesis, precognition, shadow-manipulation, empathic perception, and others.  The mechanisms are not understood.  The mutations are rare even among long-term residents of compatible worlds.

## The Fade

Paragon powers are sustained by proximity to compatible planetary energies.  When a paragon leaves the domain of their origin, the power weakens over time.  The rate of fade varies, but most paragons report noticeable degradation within weeks of leaving their system, and near-total loss within months.

Crucially, and largely unknown, the "domain" is not limited to a single planet.  A handful of compatible worlds exist for each type of mutation, scattered across the Forge.  A paragon who knows the locations of their compatible planets can plan routes that keep them "fueled."  The gaps between compatible worlds are dangerous; even if you know this secret and know of other compatible planets, you always expect to go dark before reaching the next one.

## Implications

- Paragons who wish to retain their powers while travelling must know where their refueling stops are
- The locations of compatible planets are extraordinarily valuable and dangerous information
- If those locations become known, factions will seek to control them, exploit them, or weaponize the people tied to them
- Most paragons are not scientists or scholars; they are ordinary people who stumbled into a gift and may not understand its limits until they leave home for the first time

## Known and Possible Paragon Types

Not all of these necessarily exist in the Forge.  Concepts for play.

**Clearly supernatural:**
- **Shade**: Object concealment or self-cloaking.  Eyes slide past what they hide.
- **Kinetic**: Telekinesis.  Push, pull, lift, constrict.
- **Firebrand**: Wield fiery energy.  Generate and control heat/flame.
- **Empath**: Read the intent, emotions, and inner state of nearby beings.
- **Seer**: Prophetic visions and precognition.

**Easily flavored as paragon:**
- **Looper**: Time manipulation.  Rewind moments, loop back.
- **Healer**: Supernatural healing beyond medical science.
- **Naturalist**: Communion with lifeforms.  The space druid.
- **Infiltrator**: Self-cloaking (the "personal invisibility" Shade variant).
- **Haunted**: Spirit medium.  Commune with the dead.  Tied to ghost light systems (white dwarf stars) specifically.  Their refueling stops are where the horrors live.

**Stretch candidates:**
- **Lore Hunter**: Psychometric.  Reads the history of objects and places by touch.

## Leight's Situation

Leight's Shade gift is tied to the jungle world below Paxton Station.  He has lived and worked in this system his entire career with the Cartel, and has never been far enough away for long enough to notice the fade.  He is currently unaware that leaving this system will cost him his power.

He knows of no other Shade-compatible planets.  When he discovers the limitation (likely on his first out-of-system run), finding the next compatible world will become urgent.

## Open Questions

- How quickly does the fade onset after leaving a compatible system?
- Can the fade be slowed or temporarily arrested by any means?
- How many Shade-compatible planets exist in the Forge?
- Does anyone else know about the planet below Paxton?  Does the Cartel?

## Theories (in-world, unconfirmed)

One school of thought holds that compatible planets are not naturally occurring.  The mutations may be vestiges of precursor-era biological warfare or residual contamination from ancient conflicts.  Those worlds were uninhabitable for millennia before the Exodus; by the time humanity settled them, the danger looked like a gift.  Under this theory, paragons aren't chosen.  They're contaminated in a way that happens to be useful.

## Rumors (to be discovered in play)

**The Healer's Story:** A healer-order scholar once treated the aftermath of a paragon's capture.  The paragon had fled their home system and was on the run.  Bounty hunters caught them after the power apparently faded.  Beat them.  Tortured them.  Dragged them back home.  Once back in their domain, the power returned.  The paragon broke out of prison and the healer was called to treat the hunters.  Was the paragon faking the fade the entire time, enduring the beatings to set up a devastating breakout?  Or did the power genuinely ebb and flow back to life once they were returned to compatible ground?  The healer doesn't know.  Nobody does.
