# Expedition Hover Barge

**Type:** Low-altitude transport / expedition platform
**Common Use:** [[Factions/Vanguard Recon Group|Vanguard]] deep-jungle sorties, recovery operations
**Location:** [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Orris|Orris]] surface operations

## Description

A swamp airboat reimagined for dense jungle.  The standard expedition transport at [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Celadon Terminus|Celadon Terminus]].

- **Platform:** Shallow, open-deck barge.  Crew can jump on and off easily; no doors, no hull to climb over.  Speed of deployment matters when something's coming.
- **Lift:** Small directional thrusters keep it aloft at roughly 1–2 meters.  Enough to clear root tangles, fallen logs, and low brush.  Not enough for serious deadfalls or thick vine mass.
- **Drive:** Large, conspicuous fan unit mounted aft (like a swamp boat).  Provides forward thrust.  They can move fast if they ever get a clear line; not that they often do in these trees.
- **Brush cables:** Angled wire cables from bow to a raised frame, forming a wedge.  Insulated (or sacrificial) so shock vines slide along them instead of draping across the crew.  Need regular replacement as insulation degrades.

## Operational Use

Vanguard expedition teams ride the platform to their objective.  When something's too tall for hover clearance, chainsaw crews jump off to clear it, then jump back on.  The barge keeps them mobile and off the ground where root systems and smaller fauna operate.

## Failure Modes

- **Full thruster failure:** The barge is just a flat platform sitting in the mud.  The crew walks home.  Walking home through forgespawn jungle is how people get killed.
- **Partial failure:** Lists or drags on one side.  Limps back if close enough to the wire.  Otherwise: same as above.

## Maintenance

Maintained by [[Characters/NPCs/Rowena|Rowena]]'s repair bay.  Vanguard is hard on equipment; barges come back trashed and are expected fixed yesterday.  When one's beyond repair, Rowena writes it off to [[Characters/NPCs/Marrow|Marrow]]'s salvage yard.

## "The Bulldog"

Either a specific vehicle or a model designation at Celadon Terminus.  Currently down in Rowena's bay waiting on thrusters that arrived a week late via [[Characters/Leight/Leight|Leight]].

## Notes

- Visual reference: Louisiana swamp airboat crossed with a military landing craft.
- The conspicuous fan drive means you can hear them coming from a distance.  Forgespawn don't care; they're sensor-invisible, not deaf.  But other humans in the jungle know when a barge is nearby.
