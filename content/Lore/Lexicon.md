# Lexicon

In-universe slang, patois, and terminology established in play.

## General Forge Slang

**Jin**: A lucky charm or personal token.  From djinn (Arabic), worn smooth over centuries of use until the religious meaning faded and only "lucky thing you carry" remains.  See also: [[Characters/Leight/Black Iron Totem]].

**Drift**:  Salvage.  Flotsam.  Stuff found adrift, tossed "ashore," or otherwise without clear provenance.  Like driftwood washing up.  Can refer to scrap, junk, found objects, or people operating at that social stratum.  "He's drift" = he's nobody, scrap-tier.

**Drift shop**:  A junk store, salvage market, or second-hand parts dealer.  Sells whatever washed up.  Ranges from organized storefronts to a tarp on the deck with a pile of scrap.

**Ox scrubbers**:  Slang for oxygen/CO2 scrubbers aboard ships and stations.  Nobody says "oxygen reclamation system" unless they're writing a manual.

**Caf**:  Caffeine drink.  Generic term covering everything from proper brewed coffee-analogues to synthetic caff pills dissolved in hot water.

**Cafshop**:  Any small establishment serving caf.  Not fancy.  Counter service, maybe a few stools.

**The well**:  Planetside, relative to an orbital station.  "Going down the well" = taking the shuttle to the surface.  "Up the well" = back to orbit.

## Paxton / Marigny Local

**Dip the shrine**:  Touch or acknowledge a small shrine/memorial in passing, for luck.  A habitual gesture, like crossing yourself.  Leight does this passing through R5.

## Celadon Terminus / Orris

**The watchtower's still standing**:  Morbid greeting among perimeter crews.  Meaning: nothing's changed, we're still here, the jungle hasn't swallowed it yet.  Refers to the unfinished vista tower visible above the canopy.  The day you can't see it anymore, things have gotten worse.
