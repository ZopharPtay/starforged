# The Stellar Wardens
**Type:** Founder Clan
**Colloquial:** "The Wardens"
**Leadership:** Clan chiefs or elders (assumed)

## Summary

One of the five Founder Clans that claim territories in the Forge.  The Wardens position themselves as protectors and guardians of their settled domains.  They maintain a professional military fleet and territorial holdings.

## Intelligence Bureau: Wraiths of the Elder Imperium

**Colloquial:** "The Wraiths"

The Wardens' shadow arm.  An intelligence bureau that officially doesn't exist but everyone suspects is real.  Operates embedded agents (assets) across the Forge, including within rival Clans, mercenary guilds, and criminal organizations.  Even within the Wardens, most people don't know the Wraiths' full operational picture.

## Connection to The Hulking Anguish

[[The Hulking Anguish]] was originally a Wardens fleet vessel (dispatch/VIP courier).  It was decommissioned and surplus-auctioned after a Wraith officer ([[Commander Lantern|Commander "Lantern"]]) was lost during a boarding action aboard the vessel.  The ship's pilot, [[Lt. Maren Kade (Threshold)|Lt. Maren Kade]], was also killed in the incident.
