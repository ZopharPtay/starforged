# Vanguard Recon Group

**Type:** Mercenary company (expedition security / deep-jungle operations)
**Colloquial:** "Vanguard," "the rangers"
**Leadership:** TBD (company commander, probably off-world)
**Contract Holder:** TBD (the Faction that owns the Orris extraction claim)

## Summary

Professional mercenary outfit contracted to provide expedition security for the [[Locations/The Marigny/Orris/Orris|Orris]] extraction operation.  They go outside the wire.  Escort sorties into forgespawn territory, recover lost assets, run deep-jungle operations that nobody else will touch.  They do not handle compound security (that's [[The Cartel]]'s domain) and they don't answer to anyone on the ground except their own chain of command.

## Character

- Former military, overwhelmingly.  Combat veterans who aren't ready for quiet private security.
- Adrenaline junkies.  More than a few are running on combat stims or chemical cocktails.
- Professional and good at it.  These are not mall cops looking for better pay.  They're the best jungle fighters credits can buy, and they know it.
- Insular.  They eat together, bunk together, run their own schedule.  Compound workers are invisible to them.
- They don't keep broken soldiers.  If you can't run, you're off the roster.  No exceptions.

## Relationship to Other Power Structures

- **The Company (TBD):** Signs the checks.  Vanguard answers to whoever holds the extraction contract, not to compound management.  The relationship is purely transactional.
- **[[The Cartel]]:** Mutual contempt.  The Cartel handles internal compound security; Vanguard handles the jungle.  The Cartel can't touch them, can't control their movements, can't skim their operation.  Vanguard sees the Cartel as glorified bouncers playing gangster.
- **Compound workers:** Invisible.  Vanguard doesn't interact with extraction staff unless they have to.  Workers resent them for being expensive, untouchable, and indifferent to the compound's problems.

## The Injury Clause

Vanguard's contract with the Company includes an injury/death benefit clause.  It's paid to Vanguard as an organization; whatever reaches the individual soldier is whatever fraction the company's internal policies dictate.  The Company honors the letter of the contract; replacing a merc is a line item, not a tragedy.  Vanguard discards soldiers who can't operate; the clause is severance, not care.

## Notable Members (Past and Present)
### [[Characters/NPCs/Marrow|Marrow]] - Former

Rokuro "Marrow" Ishida was Vanguard.  Three years on active expedition rotation.  Lost both legs on his last run; vine shocks, toxins, infection from carrying a dead crewmate out of deep jungle on foot.  Vanguard paid out the clause, struck him from the roster, moved on.  The Company caught him on the way down: two bottom-rung prosthetics and a salvage yard posting.  He's not Vanguard anymore.  They don't acknowledge him.

## Force Composition

- Two squads of 12 operators on Orris at any given time.  24 total.
- Self-sufficient; they don't drag civilians on operations.
- Use company vehicles (crawlers, skimmers) maintained by [[Characters/NPCs/Rowena|Rowena]]'s repair bay.  Hard on equipment.  Bring it back trashed, expect it fixed yesterday.
- When a vehicle is beyond repair, Rowena writes it off to [[Characters/NPCs/Marrow|Marrow]]'s salvage yard.  Vanguard burns through hardware fast.
