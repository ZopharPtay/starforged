# House Voss-Dern

**Type:** Banking/merchant dynasty
**Colloquial:** "The Voss-Derns"
**Influence:** Financial (kuromaku / king-maker level)

## Summary

Old money of the oldest kind.  House Voss-Dern doesn't need titles or territory; they hold paper on the people who do.  A Rothschild/Rockefeller-tier banking dynasty whose wealth predates the Exodus and whose influence spans the Forge.  They funded the wars and funded both sides.  Their power is exercised through capital: liens, credit, insurance exclusions, quiet conversations with bank managers.

They don't command fleets.  They don't need to.  The people who command fleets owe them money.

## Relationship to Other Factions

- Fund and hold debt over multiple Founder Clans (including likely the [[Stellar Wardens]])
- Operate through legitimate banking institutions, not directly
- Their displeasure manifests as financial pressure, not violence

## Connection to The Hulking Anguish

[[Stellan Voss-Dern]], a scion of the family, was romantically entangled with [[Mira Trevane]] during the ship's civilian luxury era.  When the relationship was exposed as originating from a con, the family used financial leverage to repossess the vessel and blacklist the Trevanes from operating.
