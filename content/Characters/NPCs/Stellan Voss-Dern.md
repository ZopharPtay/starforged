## Summary

A genuine, somewhat sheltered young man from one of the most powerful banking dynasties in the Forge.  Never had anyone not want something from him, which is exactly why Mira's attention felt different once it became real.  Ignored his family's insistence that she was "beneath his station" and that no good would come of it.  Everyone in old-money society saw through the Trevanes from the beginning; Stellan was the last to know.

When [[Sova Trevane|Sova]] revealed the con's origins, it destroyed the one thing he believed was true.  Whether he's since recovered, grown bitter, or still carries a torch is unknown.

## Connection to The Hulking Anguish

His family's financial pressure (calling in liens, leaning on insurers) forced the repossession and resale of the ship after the relationship collapsed.  The *Miravel's Reverie* went back on the auction block because of House Voss-Dern.
