# Ike Sutton

**Role:** Antiquarian / criminal fence (spiritual artifacts)
**Location:** [[Far Provenance]], [[Paxton Orbital Station]]
**Status:** Alive

## Summary

Proprietor of Far Provenance, a spiritual antiquarian shop on Paxton Station.  An eccentric historian who can authenticate and appraise relics from any of the Forge's faiths on the spot.  Presents as a warm, curious, slightly disorganized shopkeeper.  In reality, the shop is cover for his true pursuit: locating specific artifacts tied to a topic of religious significance.

## First Impressions

Eccentric and ill-equipped.  Doesn't look like a man running a profitable enterprise.  Mismatched clothes, cluttered workspace, no investment in presentation.  But speaks with deep expertise about anything on his shelves.  Curious toward newcomers: why are they here, who sent them, what's their interest in the sacred and obscure?

## In Business

Known as "Grudge" among couriers and fixers.  The warmth evaporates when terms are broken.  Does not accept excuses for failed deliveries or botched jobs.  The Nishka/Jabba archetype: pleasant until you owe him, then unforgiving.  His reputation in the underworld is earned.

## Deeper Layers

- Part of a deeper organization (details TBD; to be discovered through play).
- Seeking specific artifacts related to a subject of religious significance (details TBD).
- His criminal activity is mission-driven, not profit-driven.  Illegal acquisitions serve a purpose beyond greed.
- Incompetent as a businessman: his expertise is narrow and his attention is selective.  Anything not directly tied to his personal pursuit is neglected.  He cannot account for most of his stock.

## Goal

Build a relationship.  He's looking for reliable contacts: couriers, scouts, people who can move things or find things discreetly.  A courier who can make items unnoticeable would be extraordinarily useful to him.

## Hooks

- Potential patron for courier work (moving relics of dubious legality between settlements).
- His organizational connections are a thread to pull.
- The gap between his reputation (Grudge) and his actual competence (narrow, neglectful) could create interesting friction.
- His shop is vulnerable to selective theft, but discovering this requires close interaction.
