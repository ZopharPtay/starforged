# Commander "Lantern"
**Rank:** Commander, Intelligence Division
**Faction:** Wraiths of the Elder Imperium ([[Stellar Wardens]] intelligence bureau)
**Status:** Deceased (executed after failed ransom)

## Summary

An intelligence officer whose real name is buried under layers of classification.  Known only by callsign, rank, and service number.  Carried, at all times, a data chip containing the Wraiths' asset network: names, covers, and locations of operatives embedded across the Forge.

During the boarding of [[First/Characters/The Hulking Anguish/The Hulking Anguish|The Hulking Anguish]], Lantern stashed the chip somewhere in the passenger compartment before being taken.  The Wardens refused the ransom.  Lantern was executed.  The Wraiths assumed the chip was lost with their officer.

## The Data Chip

- Contains the Wraiths' asset network: embedded operatives, their cover identities, and locations
- Some of those assets are now prominent figures operating under new identities
- If surfaced, it would unravel political mysteries of recent years and expose powerful people
- The protagonist is unaware of its existence
- Someone out there may know or suspect the chip still exists
