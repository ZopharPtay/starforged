# Mira Trevane

**Role:** Crew/talent aboard [[Characters/The Hulking Anguish/The Hulking Anguish|The Hulking Anguish]] (civilian luxury era)
**Status:** Alive (operating as a grifter/con artist somewhere in the Forge)

## Summary

Daughter of [[Sova Trevane]].  Smart, charming, complicit in her mother's schemes.  Played hostess on the charter yacht *Miravel's Reverie* (named after her, the "up-classed" version of her real name).  Trained to be generous with young men of means in hopes of hooking a wealthy suitor.

The con turned real when she fell genuinely for a client.  When Sova was banned from the family's company and Mira chose silence over defending her mother, Sova retaliated by exposing the original scheme.  Mira lost the relationship, the ship, and her mother in one stroke.

Street-smart and not entirely innocent.  She landed on her feet and is out there somewhere in the Forge, likely running smaller cons on her own.  Could be a future connection or complication.

## Legacy on the Ship

- The greenwall/hydroponics bay was her personal touch (making the ship feel alive)
- The ship's luxury-era name (*Miravel's Reverie*) was a version of her name
- Captain 3 kept the greenwall and repurposed it for vegetables
