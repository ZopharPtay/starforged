# Flint

**Role:** Independent mechanic / black-market hardware specialist
**Pronouns:** They/them
**Faction:** None (formerly used by [[The Cartel]]; burned by them)
**Status:** Alive
**Connection Rank:** Dangerous

## Summary

Early 30s, non-binary.  Small, quick, covered in plasma-welding burn scars on their forearms.  Goes by Flint; won't give a family name.  The name they won't give is Wolfe: [[Vuldar Wolfe|Warden]] was their younger brother, though almost no one knows it.  Flint didn't set out to join the Cartel.  They offered to do "a job", meaning to help Warden, never grasping how deep he already was or how little he wanted out.  Flint got entangled in the process and couldn't get clear, though they never stopped angling for a way out, for both of them.  That constant flight-risk restlessness made them a tiresome liability, and it was bound to draw the wrong attention.  Eventually the Cartel pinned a weapons-trafficking charge on them and cut them loose, as much to be rid of the nuisance as anything else.  Did time.  Now works alone out of a rough port shop.

Doesn't trust anyone.  Tolerates Nassir because "at least you never pretended to be my friend while you were screwing me."  Holds a cold grudge against the Cartel but isn't stupid enough to act on it alone.

## Family: Warden

- [[Vuldar Wolfe]] ("Warden") was Flint's younger brother.  The relationship is a closely-held secret; Nassir doesn't know, and few if any do.
- Warden fell in with The Cartel early and willingly.  By nature he was an enforcer who oriented on a charge and protected it absolutely, by directive rather than sentiment (the Amos/Timmy model from The Expanse).  He didn't want out and "didn't need saving."
- Flint offered to do "a job" meaning to help him, misjudged how deep he was and how little he wanted leaving, and got entangled themselves with no way clear.
- What outsiders read as "they never got along" was sibling friction: an older sibling grinding against a reckless younger one who refused to be rescued.
- The cruel asymmetry: Flint gave up a clean life trying to pull Warden out and never managed it.  Warden died doing the only thing he ever did, guarding a near-stranger ([[Characters/Leight/Leight|Nassir]]) because he was told to.  Flint will likely never know that's how it went.
- Flint learned of Warden's death during Nassir's visit to the shop (Session 2), at the "Knew?" beat, and covered the reaction fast.

## Relationship with Nassir

- Nassir used Flint for off-the-books damage repairs during his Cartel days (hiding evidence of messy jobs before reporting back)
- They have a transactional history, not a friendship
- Mutual self-interest: Nassir needs a mechanic who won't talk; Flint needs paying work that doesn't come with Cartel strings
- Tension: Nassir was part of the machine that burned Flint.  That hasn't been addressed.
- Both knew [[Vuldar Wolfe]] ("Warden") - but as far as Nassir knows, only as a mutual Cartel contact.  Nassir does not know Warden was Flint's brother (Flint hides the Wolfe name).  When Nassir said "you knew Warden" in the shop, he was understating it far more than he realized.

## Current Situation

- Recently out of lockup, assumed monitored by authorities
- The Cartel won't use them anymore (too "hot")
- Running an independent shop, probably struggling for clients who aren't scared off by the Cartel association
- Needs work.  Nassir showing up with a half-dead SH-56 is a payday and a project.
