# Flint

**Role:** Independent mechanic / black-market hardware specialist
**Pronouns:** They/them
**Faction:** None (formerly used by [[The Cartel]]; burned by them)
**Status:** Alive
**Connection Rank:** Dangerous

## Summary

Early 30s, non-binary.  Small, quick, covered in plasma-welding burn scars on their forearms.  Goes by Flint; won't give a family name.  Used to do black-market modifications for whoever paid.  The Cartel used them for quiet hardware jobs, then pinned a weapons-trafficking charge on them when they needed a distraction.  Did time.  Now works alone out of a rough port shop.

Doesn't trust anyone.  Tolerates Nassir because "at least you never pretended to be my friend while you were screwing me."  Holds a cold grudge against the Cartel but isn't stupid enough to act on it alone.

## Relationship with Nassir

- Nassir used Flint for off-the-books damage repairs during his Cartel days (hiding evidence of messy jobs before reporting back)
- They have a transactional history, not a friendship
- Mutual self-interest: Nassir needs a mechanic who won't talk; Flint needs paying work that doesn't come with Cartel strings
- Tension: Nassir was part of the machine that burned Flint.  That hasn't been addressed.
- Both knew [[Vuldar Wolfe]] ("Warden"), the friend who died to buy Nassir's freedom

## Current Situation

- Recently out of lockup, assumed monitored by authorities
- The Cartel won't use them anymore (too "hot")
- Running an independent shop, probably struggling for clients who aren't scared off by the Cartel association
- Needs work.  Nassir showing up with a half-dead SH-56 is a payday and a project.
