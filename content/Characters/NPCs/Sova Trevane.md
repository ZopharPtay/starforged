# Sova Trevane

**Role:** Captain/Owner of [[The Hulking Anguish]] (civilian luxury era)
**Status:** Unknown (likely destitute or deceased)

## Summary

A hard woman shaped by a string of bad marriages and worse luck.  Sova bought the _Hulking Anguish_ at military surplus auction and refit it as a luxury charter yacht, renaming it *Miravel's Reverie* after her daughter [[Mira Trevane|Mira]].  Her entire plan hinged on using the charter business to introduce Mira to wealthy clients and land a marriage that would elevate them both.

Charming in a slightly desperate way.  Not wealthy but good at performing wealth.  Ran the business side: bookings, glad-handing clients, managing appearances.  Installed a Passenger Comfort Monitoring Suite with full camera and sensor coverage throughout the VIP areas.  Whether this was purely for service quality or also for insurance/leverage is left ambiguous.

When Mira's relationship with a client turned genuine and Sova was pushed out, she torpedoed the relationship with blackmail rather than lose her stake.  The fallout cost both of them everything.  The ship was seized through legal pressure from the suitor's family.

## Legacy on the Ship

- Refit the ship to luxury standard (galley, entertainment suite, greenwall, PCMS)
- Named it *Miravel's Reverie*
- The PCMS camera/sensor array remains integrated into the ship's systems
