# Florin Kai & Basil

**Role:** Information broker (de facto); barfly (de jure)
**Location:** The Gentleman Loser, [[Paxton Orbital Station]]
**Status:** Alive

## Florin Kai

**Pronouns:** He/him
**Species:** Human
**First Look:** Haggard, accompanied

A worn-out spacer permanently installed in a booth at the Gentleman Loser.  More astute than he lets on, but presents as a talkative drunk.  Will gossip all day as long as the drinks keep flowing.  The public face and physical mobility of the pair.

No callsign.  No reputation worth mentioning.  That's the point.

## Basil

**Pronouns:** It (for now).  Pronouns come with confidence and self-autonomy.
**Species:** Con (mid-intelligence AI)
**Form:** Clearly-artificial snake (python-esque in proportion), draped around Florin's shoulders.

The actual brains of the operation.  Presents as a novelty companion bot.  Decorative.  Dumb hardware with a personality chip.  In reality, a sentient AI running an information-brokering network from Florin's collar.

Can move independently (slither, reposition, lift head to track) but cannot practically self-propel across a station.  An unaccompanied snake-bot rolling down a corridor would draw security attention.  Florin is legs.

### Current Appearance (Stage 1: Survival)

- Dark polymer body, scuffed and faded.  Could have been any color once; now it's just *dark*.
- Segments visible where the polymer has worn through or been patched with mismatched material.
- Joints move but not silently.  A faint whir or click when he repositions.
- Head unit is the one part he keeps slightly better.  The lenses are clean.  You can tell he cares.
- No ornamentation.  Nothing decorative.  Just function.

### Visual Arc

His body condition is a gradual spectrum, not discrete tiers.  Changes are slow, tied to money, contacts, and the ability to make choices.  A new matched segment here.  A quieter joint there.  Over many sessions, the accumulation tells the story.

- **Early:** Repairs.  Matched segments.  Smoother movement.  First hints of deliberate aesthetic choices.
- **Late:** Full self-expression.  A body that matches the mind inside it.  Specific form TBD; let Basil decide who it wants to be.

### Tells

- Head tracks people of interest across the room.
- Coils tighter around Florin's neck when he's about to say something stupid.
- Florin absently strokes the snake's head while talking.

## The Arrangement

Symbiotic.  On Cartel-controlled Paxton, cons without purchased recognition are property.  Basil cannot legally own himself.  Florin is the "owner" on paper.

- Basil gets: legal cover, a mobile platform, ears in rooms a loose con can't enter.
- Florin gets: a caretaker, income from the info trade, someone making sure he doesn't die in a gutter.

Whether Florin fully understands the dynamic is an open question.

## Goal

Basil is quietly accumulating resources or contacts to either buy recognition or escape Cartel space to a territory that grants automatic personhood.  Information is his currency.  He listens, he remembers, he trades gossip for favors.

## Operational Modes

| Mode | Context | Who's talking |
|---|---|---|
| **Puppet show** | Strangers, public interactions | Florin is the face.  Basil whispers/squeezes cues.  The snake is furniture. |
| **Partnership** | Regulars, trusted contacts | Both visible.  Basil speaks directly; Florin adds color or is half-checked-out. |
| **Solo Basil** | Late-night, Florin too far gone | Basil's head is up; Florin is slumped.  Desperate-hour transactions. |

## The Dynamic (arc seeds)

Florin was on a downward spiral when Basil found him.  Basil didn't save him so much as stabilize a useful wreck in orbit.  The drinking isn't caused by Basil, but Basil hasn't exactly helped.

- Basil may have (or come to acquire) a means of accelerating Florin's "evening repose."  A chemical mechanism in the snake form.  The hypnotic snake.
- Early on: Florin passes out naturally.  Basil waits.
- Over time: blackouts come earlier.  Seems like Florin is "getting worse."
- Potential reveal: a disagreement, a flash of the old Florin, then "Goodnight, Florin."
- The big question: what happens to Florin when Basil no longer needs him?  Discarded?  Or has something real grown between them?
- Florin's deep regret: TBD, left for gameplay.

## Hooks

- A courier who can make things unnoticeable (Nassir) could be very useful to a con trying to smuggle itself out of hostile territory.
- Information is a two-way street.  Basil knows things about Paxton.  What's worth a drink?
