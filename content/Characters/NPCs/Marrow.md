# Marrow

**Real Name:** Rokuro Ishida
**Role:** Salvage yard operator, [[Celadon Terminus]]
**Pronouns:** He/him
**Faction:** Extraction operation (employee; quietly hostile)
**Status:** Alive
**Connection Rank:** TBD

## Summary

Late 40s, maybe 50.  Former expedition crew.  Built like he used to be bigger; the frame is still there but the mass has settled into something leaner and harder.  Runs the salvage yard at the south end of the compound: a tarp-roofed dirt lot with a log floor and a warehouse of stripped parts.  Everyone calls him Marrow.  Nobody planetside knows him as anything else.

Two cybernetic legs from mid-thigh down.  Bottom-rung company issue; the kind of prosthetics that say "we fulfilled our legal obligation" and nothing more.  He's modified them extensively on his own time with yard tools.  Custom cleated soles for the log flooring; he moves across his yard with a confidence that makes everyone else look like they're on ice.  The legs clack differently than boots.  You hear him coming.

## The Old Glory

He was expedition crew.  Three years on active rotation before the injury.  He'll tell you about every run except the last one.  The terrain, the wildlife, the close calls, the camaraderie.  He talks about it like an old marine who forgot how much combat sucks and only remembers the highs.  The boasting is loud and practiced and constant.

He never tells the Corey story unprompted.  Everyone on the compound knows it anyway.  New workers get told in a low voice: "don't ask about his legs."

## The Corey Story (not public knowledge in full)

An expedition went bad when a Cat got the crew, the hallucinogenic breath took them.  Marrow and a crewmate named Corey were the last two moving.  Marrow carried Corey out of the deep jungle on foot, his legs already dying from accumulated vine shocks, toxins, and infections.  Drugged by the cat, hallucinating, pushing through on pure stubborn refusal to leave his partner.

When he got back to camp and set Corey down they told him Corey was already dead; probably since the canopy, maybe since the first breath.

The company gave him a commendation, two cheap legs, and the salvage yard to rot in.  The commendation is face-down on a shelf somewhere.  He won't take it down.  He won't talk about what it's actually for.

## The Yard

- Tarp roof stretched from a decommissioned shuttle wing mounted on posts; tarps radiate outward, expanding coverage as needed
- Log floor: split trunks from perimeter clearing, gaps packed with mud.  Char marks and vine scarring still visible.  Sturdy enough for heavy equipment.
- A cluster of equipment in various stages of teardown; one vehicle at a time, not rows of dead machines.  Generators, comms units, whatever comes in.
- Warehouse at one end (welded containers) for sorted, shelved salvage
- His domain.  He works at his own pace.  Nothing here is urgent until it is.
- Not a junkyard; he strips efficiently and the hulls get repurposed (perimeter material, housing, night-shift fuel).  The yard is working space, not a graveyard.

## The Mark Three

Marrow is building an expedition barge from scratch.  His design.  The one the company should have built instead of the Mark Twos the Vanguard is still flying.  He knows every standard failure case because he nearly died on one.  The skirt actuators, the brush cable placement, the lift thruster clearance; he has opinions on all of it, loudly, to anyone who'll listen.

He'll never fly it.  His legs aren't jungle-rated anymore and there's no crew and no mission.  Everyone knows this.  He knows this.  He's building it anyway.  It's his expertise given physical form; proof he was more than a body they used up and discarded.

Some Vanguard crews sneak down to look at it and ask questions.  The company won't fund a redesign because the current barges are "good enough."

## The Motorcycle

A ground bike.  Two wheels, an engine, a frame.  He saw one on an old trideo and thought they looked cool.  Completely impractical on a planet with no roads.  He's building it from scratch out of salvage anyway.

He doesn't justify this one to anyone.  It's just his.

## The Side Work

Rowena handles company vehicles and won't touch personal kit on company time.  Marrow will fix your hand terminal, your bunk heater, your comms unit, whatever.  Barter economy.  That's how he knows everyone on the compound without being social; they come to him.  He doesn't have to go anywhere.

## The Side Operation

The company writes off wrecked vehicles to the salvage yard.  Nobody inventories what's in the floorboard cracks.  On rare occasion, Marrow finds things that were dropped by the crews.  The standard "fell between the seat cushion" drift, usually but occasionally something else: a chip of Iron, a fragment of precursor material that "got lost in the excitement" when the crew ran for the wire.

He sells parts off-books to people like [[Characters/NPCs/Flint|Flint]].  Below market, no questions.  Every shipment that goes up the well without passing through official channels is another papercut for the company that bought his silence with minimum-viable legs.

He's not trying to bring them down.  He's not foolish.  But he's making sure they don't get everything they paid for.

## The Grudge

- **Resolve a dispute:** The compensation he never got.  The posting he deserved.  The "thank you for your service, here's a broom closet" that he swallowed and has never digested.
- **Sabotage the operation:**  Skimming profit, selling off salvage that should go back into the operation's supply chain.  Occasionally pocketing precursor material that nobody knows was there.  Slow, patient, deniable revenge.

## The Precursor Connection

He doesn't know what the Iron is, exactly.  Doesn't know the vault's full story.  He just knows certain wrecks come back with strange black metal in them, and certain buyers pay extremely well for it without asking where it came from.  He's a source node in a supply chain that probably routes through [[Locations/The Marigny/Paxton Orbital Station/Far Provenance|Far Provenance]] on Paxton.  He doesn't know that and doesn't need to.
