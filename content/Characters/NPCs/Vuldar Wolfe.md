# Vuldar Wolfe

**Callsign:** Warden
**Pronouns:** He/Him
**Faction:** Formerly [[The Cartel]]
**Status:** Dead

## Summary

Died guarding Nassir on his last job for The Cartel, which bought Nassir's freedom.  Known to both Nassir and [[Flint]].  How he died and what the "last job" entailed are yet to be established in play.

By nature an enforcer in the Amos/Timmy mold (The Expanse): he oriented on a charge and protected it with total, literal loyalty, by directive rather than sentiment.  Came up hard and fell in with [[The Cartel]] early and willingly; it gave that nature a place to live.  His callsign, "Warden," fits exactly: a keeper, a guard.  He guarded Nassir because he was told to.  That was all there was to it.  Not friendship, not redemption.

## Relationships

- **Nassir:** Died guarding him, and that death bought Nassir's freedom (Epic vow: Pay a Debt).  But the loyalty was duty, not affection; Warden protected whoever he was assigned to.  A debt Nassir can never repay, owed to a man who felt nothing personal about paying it.
- **Flint:** His older sibling, a closely-held secret (Nassir doesn't know).  Warden was Cartel early and deep and didn't want out.  Flint got entangled trying to help him and couldn't escape.  Outsiders read their constant friction as bad blood; it was siblings.

## Open Questions

- How exactly did he die?
- What was the last job, and how did guarding Nassir translate into buying his freedom?
- What was Vuldar's role in The Cartel? (Working answer: enforcer, assigned as a guard or escort.)
