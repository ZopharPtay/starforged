  A mother (captain/owner)/daughter(crew/talent) team in dire straights purchased the ship from surplus and fix it up.  [[Sova Trevane]] has had a _rough_ life and a rougher (string of?) marriage(s) and is absolutely determined that her daughter will get better opportunities.  She renames the ship *Miravel's Reverie* after her daughter [[Mira Trevane|Mira]] (the "up-classed" version of her real name).  She sees this as the chance to introduce Mira to the upper crust and seduce a rich suitor.  The daughter is taught and encouraged to be generous with the young men in hopes of hooking one.

# Narrative

[[Sova Trevane]] bought the ship at surplus auction for next to nothing.  Nobody else wanted the bad-luck military boat, but Sova saw opportunity: a solid hull, cheap price, and a cabin that could be dressed up to impress people who'd never look too closely at the bones underneath.  She renamed it *Miravel's Reverie* after her daughter [[Mira Trevane|Mira]], the "up-classed" version of the girl's real name, and set about turning it into a luxury charter yacht.

Years of work and reinvested profits transformed the interior.  A proper galley, entertainment suite, lush greenwall in the VIP cabin (Mira's personal touch), and a custom Passenger Comfort Monitoring Suite wired throughout the passenger areas: cameras, temperature sensors, vibration monitors.  The stated purpose was service quality.  Whether Sova had other intentions for the footage is left to interpretation.

The scheme was simple: charter flights for the wealthy, with Mira playing gracious hostess.  The plan was always to hook a rich young man.  Mira was taught, willing, and good at it.  Then she fell for [[Stellan Voss-Dern]], a scion of [[House Voss-Dern]], one of the most powerful banking dynasties in the Forge.  What started as another mark turned genuine.

Everyone in old-money circles saw through the Trevanes from the start.  Stellan was the last to know.  His family tolerated it as a phase, then started applying pressure once he talked about a future.  Sova, not realizing the con had stopped being a con for her daughter, kept pushing: hints about investments, comments about "when you two are settled."  The Voss-Derns declared Sova unwelcome.  Mira, faced with a choice, stayed quiet rather than defend her mother.

Sova took the silence as betrayal and went nuclear.  She went to the family, told them how it all started, and may have leveraged PCMS footage as blackmail.  Everything collapsed.  Stellan was blindsided.  Mira lost him.  Sova lost Mira.  House Voss-Dern called in the liens, leaned on insurers, and the *Miravel's Reverie* went back on the auction block.

Mira is out there somewhere in the Forge, running smaller cons, street-smart and scarred.  Sova is a bitter old woman, likely destitute, possibly dead.  The ship moved on to its next owner with luxury fittings still bolted to military bones and a camera system buried so deep in the wiring that nobody's bothered to pull it out.
