## Overview

A mid-size interstellar vessel built on the SH-56 Patroclus chassis (universally called "the Lurch" by its crews, thanks to its gangly spider-leg landing gear).  Originally configured as the SH-56V VIP/courier variant.  The Patroclus has been in service for generations; the cargo variant (SH-56C) is the workhorse of the Forge and parts are in every junkyard.  Think C-40/C-130 scale: crew of up to three, with the remaining interior configurable for either VIP passenger comfort or palletized freight.  After three owners and decades of mixed use, it's now an idiosyncratic Frankenstein ship with military bones, faded luxury ghosts, and half-finished restoration work.

## History (Bucket of Bolts)

Three captains before the protagonist.  Each left their mark:

1. **[[Captain 1 - The Military Era]]** - [[Lt. Maren Kade (Threshold)|Lt. Maren Kade "Threshold"]], [[Stellar Wardens]] fleet pilot.  The ship shuttled VIPs and classified cargo.  Ended when boarders killed Threshold and abducted [[Commander Lantern|Commander "Lantern"]] (Wraiths intelligence officer).  Ship decommissioned and auctioned cheap.
2. **[[Captain 2 - The Civilian Luxury Era]]** - [[Sova Trevane]] (mother) and [[Mira Trevane]] (daughter), a charter team.  Rebranded as *Miravel's Reverie*.  Luxury fittings added.  Ended when a con-turned-real-romance collapsed and the suitor's family seized the ship.
3. **[[Captain 3 - The Restorer]]** - [[Oren Herschel]], retired combat engineer.  Served on the SH-56C cargo variant his whole career (as did his father and grandfather).  Stripped the luxury, started restoring it to the freighter he remembered, never finished.  Passed away with no next of kin; his property was sold as a lot and the ship ended up at resale.

## Notable Features

- **Internal airlock** between flight deck and passenger/cargo areas (accessibility mod from Captain 1, never removed)
- **Over-pressurized flight deck** (same origin; ears pop when the doors seal)
- **Undocumented false panel** in the secure hold (standard classified-courier feature, missing from civilian paperwork; protagonist knows about this and uses it)
- **Hidden data chip** somewhere in the passenger compartment (Wraiths asset network; protagonist is unaware)
- **Segmented landing gear** that unfolds like gangly spider legs
- **Vehicle Bay** containing a non-functional hoverbike with sentimental value
- **Oren's build log** hidden somewhere aboard (undiscovered; documents everything he did and left alone)
- **Oren's heir** is out there, serving on an SH-56, quietly looking for his grandfather's ship

## Name History

- **Military era:** *Hulking Anguish* (original fleet designation)
- **Civilian luxury era:** *Miravel's Reverie* (named after [[Mira Trevane|Mira]], the daughter)
- **Restorer era:** Reverted to *Hulking Anguish* ("That's not her *name*.")
- **Current:** *The Hulking Anguish*
