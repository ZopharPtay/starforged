## Overview

A mid-size interstellar vessel built on the SH-56 Patroclus chassis (universally called "the Lurch" by its crews, thanks to its gangly spider-leg landing gear).  Originally configured as the SH-56V VIP/courier variant.  The Patroclus has been in service for generations; the cargo variant (SH-56C) is the workhorse of the Forge and parts are in every junkyard.  Crew of up to three, with the remaining interior configurable for either VIP passenger comfort or palletized freight.  Smaller than a Firefly-class (crew of 3, not 9; no shuttlecraft) but substantially larger than a single-deck fighter or runabout; think somewhere between the Mandalorian's Razor Crest and Serenity.  Two decks, four cabins, a partially segmented cargo hold, and a belly-mounted vehicle bay.  After three owners and decades of mixed use, it's now an idiosyncratic Frankenstein ship with military bones, faded luxury ghosts, and half-finished restoration work.

## History (Bucket of Bolts)

Three captains before the protagonist.  Each left their mark:

1. **[[Captain 1 - The Military Era]]** - [[Lt. Maren (Threshold) Kade|Lt. Maren Kade "Threshold"]], [[Stellar Wardens]] fleet pilot.  The ship shuttled VIPs and classified cargo.  Ended when boarders killed Threshold and abducted [[Commander Lantern|Commander "Lantern"]] (Wraiths intelligence officer).  Ship decommissioned and auctioned cheap.
2. **[[Captain 2 - The Civilian Luxury Era]]** - [[Sova Trevane]] (mother) and [[Mira Trevane]] (daughter), a charter team.  Rebranded as *Miravel's Reverie*.  Luxury fittings added.  Ended when a con-turned-real-romance collapsed and the suitor's family seized the ship.
3. **[[Captain 3 - The Restorer]]** - [[Oren Herschel]], retired combat engineer.  Served on the SH-56C cargo variant his whole career (as did his father and grandfather).  Stripped the luxury, started restoring it to the freighter he remembered, never finished.  Passed away with no next of kin; his property was sold as a lot and the ship ended up at resale.

- **Internal airlock** between flight deck and passenger/cargo areas (accessibility mod from Captain 1, never removed)

- **Undocumented false panel** in the cargo hold ramp (standard classified-courier feature, missing from civilian paperwork; protagonist is unaware)

- **Vehicle Bay** belly-mounted; currently empty.  Non-functional hoverbike stashed on Paxton, not yet aboard.
- **Oren's build log** hidden somewhere aboard (undiscovered; documents everything he did and left alone)
- **Oren's heir** is out there, serving on an SH-56, quietly looking for his grandfather's ship

## Vehicle Bay Design

Belly-mounted bay accessed externally via an elevator platform that lowers between the spider-leg landing gear.  Internal access from the main deck via a hatch/ladder.

**SH-56 standard (milspec):** Sized for two tactical vehicles side by side on the elevator.  Rapid deployment: elevator drops, vehicles roll off, elevator rises, ship lifts.  Workbench/prep space in the bay proper above the elevator footprint.

**SH-56V / VIP era:** One bay still configured for a vehicle (armored dignitary transport).  The other side reconfigured with luggage racking, secure storage, and cradles for personal watercraft/toys.  Floor partitioned with different tie-down patterns.  Workbench became a valet staging area.

**Oren era:** Luxury racking stripped.  Workbench restored.  One side held his project hoverbike; the other was half-cleared with his own tooling.

**Current (Leight):** Empty.  Two open bays, mismatched floor hardware from different eras, a workbench (possibly still has some of Oren's tools/parts).  Elevator presumably functional (military-spec mechanical; robust) but untested by Leight.

## Cargo Hold

Partially segmented main hold with side bays/compartments.  The main area is open (cargo rail system two-thirds rebuilt by Oren).  The sides are broken into alcoves that have been repurposed through eras:

- **Milspec:** Crew gear lockers, weapon lockup, equipment staging
- **Luxury era:** Repurposed or removed; some anchor points remain
- **Oren era:** Restored lockers for tool and project storage; most of his hand-machined parts and supplies lived here
- **Current:** Whatever Oren left behind; partially organized, partially mysterious
