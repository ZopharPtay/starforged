# Black Iron Totem

[[Characters/Leight/Leight|Leight]]'s iron.  He calls it his jin.

## Description

A black iron inner wheel bearing, roughly 1.5" in diameter.  Domed on one side (cabochon profile), flatter on the other.  The outer race has rollers (a mix of balls and cylinders) partially exposed on the outside face.  Two adjacent rollers are missing, leaving a gap wide enough to thread a cord through as a lanyard hole.  The whole assembly still spins but rough; whatever machine this came out of took massive damage.  The surface is scored, micro-fractured, impact-marked.  Despite all that, it doesn't corrode, doesn't wear further, and stays cool to the touch.

Heavier than it looks.

## What Leight Knows

It's his lucky piece.  He's had it long enough that the origin is hazy even to him.  He swears on it when he means a promise.  He fidgets with it constantly.  He does not know it's black iron, precursor-forged, or "living metal attuned to the hidden depths of the universe."  It's just drift that felt right in his hand and never left his pocket.

## Fidget Vocabulary

- Rolling the exposed balls/cylinders with his thumb (the main idle motion)
- Spinning the outer housing around the inner race (gritty, satisfying resistance)
- Finger spins through the axle bore
- Spinning it like a top on the domed side (bar tops, seatbacks, his thigh)
- Knuckle walks using the irregular shape for grip points
- Palming it, vanishing it, producing it (sleight-of-hand; "the traditional manner")

The physical trick work is what earned him the name "Leight."  Everyone who knows him has seen him do it.  Nobody thinks twice about it.
